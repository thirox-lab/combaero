- **Rich NH3 is decomposed in `combustion_equilibrium` (#1314).** NH3 decomposition, 2 NH3 = N2 + 3 H2, is now an equilibrium reaction of the joint reforming + water-gas-shift solve wherever the burned state holds NH3, with N in the element balances and N2 no longer inert there. Its exact derivatives enter `combustion_equilibrium_and_jacobian`'s implicit-function solve. `reforming_equilibrium` and `reforming_equilibrium_adiabatic` equilibrate it too, also without a hydrocarbon.
  - **Before:** the NH3 that complete combustion left unburnt passed through: NH3/O2/N2 0.40/0.13/0.47 at 300 K, 1 bar came out at 1642.2 K with 0.217 NH3, where Cantera gives 1369.2 K and 9.5e-6 (+273 K); NH3/air at phi 1.2 was 51 K hot, CH4 + NH3 rich 34 K, and a cold, pressurised very rich burn 544 K.
  - **Now:** within 2e-5 K and 4e-9 in mole fraction of Cantera on the same species and NASA-9 data, including at 600-850 K and 20-50 bar, where equilibrium keeps 2-14 % NH3 (within 0.04 K where Cantera keeps a 1e-6 trace of O2, which no reaction of the model holds). Every state without NH3 is bit-identical to before, and so are lean burns at phi 3e-3 to 0.9994. Outside that band complete combustion leaves a trace of NH3 unburnt (the phi = 0 softmin below phi 3e-3, up to 1e-5 of the moles; the phi = 1 softmin above phi 0.9994), and that trace is now cracked beside the O2, as a hydrocarbon trace already is reformed: T moves by -2.9e-2 to +5.9e-3 K, dT/dX_NH3 by about 10 %, and the NH3 and H2 entries of the composition Jacobian change by O(1) (at phi 1e-6, dX_NH3/dX_NH3 0.50 -> 0.17).
  - **Known limit:** NH3 synthesis from N2 + H2 is on only where the burned state holds more than 1e-10 NH3, so an NH3 trace crossing that switches it on in a step: up to 1.05e-3 NH3 and +1.64 K for H2 at phi 4 and 100 bar (+0.44 K for CH4 at phi 2, 100 bar; +3e-4 K at phi 1.3, 1 bar). Always-on NH3 synthesis, with the methanation of #1241, is planned for 0.13.0.
