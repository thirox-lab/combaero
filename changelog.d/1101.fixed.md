- **`FlowNetwork.to_dict` saves each node's and element's `initial_guess` (#1101),** and `from_dict` restores it, so a reloaded network starts the solver from the same x0. Before, the guesses were dropped; residuals and Jacobian were already identical.
  - Only the user's guesses are saved. The warm-start seeds an `EjectorElement` writes itself (port chamber pressures, `P_jct`, port-chain flows) are left out, and the reloaded ejector seeds them again on `resolve_topology`. A guess the user set in place of one of those seeds is saved and still takes precedence after the reload.
  - The GUI saves its own schema and is unaffected.
