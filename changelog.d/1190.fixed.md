- **A cold call on a cooled channel returns the first fall even where its flux bracket straddles a fold (#1190).** `fanno_rayleigh_channel_flow` without a warm start (`G_guess <= 0`) Brent-solved the first ln-2 bracket of flux whose exit-pressure excess went from positive to non-positive. On a cooled duct whose needed drive folds, that bracket can hold a fall, a rise and a later fall, and Brent returned the third crossing, so the flux was not the first fall from zero flow and a warm start from below it returned another. Example (dry air, haaland, static exit): Pt0 199111.5 Pa, 868.17 -> 348.98 K, L 0.09662 m, D 9.1945 mm, whose needed drive peaks at 2.152 Pa near 0.0161 Gc: at 2.145 Pa the cold call gave 0.02118 Gc against a first fall at 0.01502 Gc, and 81 of 200 drives between the fold's valley and peak were wrong.
  - **Now:** on a cooled duct (Tt_exit < Tt0) a bracket whose ends straddle zero is split into two halves of log G, each given #1167's Hermite test of its ends' values and slopes; the root is solved in the first half that straddles zero or whose minimum reaches zero. The ends' slopes alone (one part) were not enough: over 46k random cooled cold calls (this PR's sweeps and its review's random, phase-swept and small-diameter sets) they left 34 straddling folds on a later crossing that two halves fix; three, four or twelve parts fix no more and cost more. The whole bracket's root is kept wherever the half holds it (exact when the half holds one crossing; with several, the whole-bracket root and the half's are both among them, and over 23k review calls keeping it never changed a verdict), so a bracket with no turn returns exactly what it did before.
  - **Measured** against a fine-grid first fall bisected 60 times: the issue's duct 0/200 wrong (was 81/200); the #1167 reviewer's random cooled sweeps (seed 7, 465 drives, and seed 23, 2254) 0 wrong (seed 23 was 4), and 0 of 5926 further drives placed in their fold bands (11 before). On a held-out seed (1500 ducts, 14375 drives) 104 misses (91 wrong roots, 13 read choked) became 28: 1 is the root's rounding 1.4e-4 Pa below a fold's peak, on the first branch, and 27 are cases this change does not touch, identical before: 10 drives on one duct whose dip lies inside a bracket with both ends positive (the limit #1167 documents), 1 on a fold below the scan's starting flux (e^-7 Gc), and 16 on one strongly cooled duct whose march does not reach zero drive at zero flow (3 Pa there).
  - **Unchanged:** heated and adiabatic ducts (1800 cold calls: same flux, same marches), and every cooled call whose answer did not change: its flux is bit-identical (22928 of 23020 cooled sweep calls; each of the other 92 was wrong before). F and J are bit-identical on all 2544 fresh evaluations of the 16 stress families (seeds 0:40, x0 and three perturbations, warm starts dropped).
  - **Near a fold's peak** the excess is flat and the first-fall root is defined only to the march's rounding: there a call can land on the rising crossing of a dip narrower than that, up to 1.5e-4 off the first fall at 2e-8 Pa below a peak (one review call; the same before).
  - **Cost:** only on cooled calls that meet a straddling bracket in the cold scan: about 2-5 more marches there (the midpoint's value and the slopes), more where a half's minimum is searched. Over 23020 random cooled cold calls: +1.7 marches on average (+5.1 %, from 37.9), none on 61 %, at most 46. A warm call is unchanged unless its bracket fails and it falls back to the cold scan, which then pays the same: 13-81 of 3000 warm calls with guesses 0.97-1.03 of the root, up to 32 more marches (with three parts the review measured up to 52).
