- **New validation dataset: rib heat transfer and friction in a high-aspect-ratio wedge passage.** `validation/cooling/data/naik2014/` holds Naik, Retzko, Gritsch and Sedlov (2014), GT2014-25841. It is an Alstom trailing-edge passage, triangular, W/H 6.64, with 45 deg ribs on both wide walls. It gives Nu/Nu0 (Fig. 16), Fanning f/f0 over Petukhov (Fig. 17) and the performance index (Fig. 18) at Re 22k, 60k and 145k, for angled-tapered, broken and chevron ribs. The extraction record is `validation/cooling/extractions/naik_2014_te_wedge_ribs.md`.
  - **Scored as cross-source accuracy, out of every set's validity box.** The angled-tapered Nu/Nu0 is scored on 3 points, of which 1 is cleanly sampled, so each result is an upper bound on the error. The sets read: `han_park_1988_angled` -14.8% (1 of 3 points inside the paper's 9.5% band), `rallabandi_2009_high_re` +24.6%, `han_zhang_lee_1991_45par` +34.2%, `han_1989_narrow_channel` +45.1%. Nothing is fitted to it.
  - **Friction is not scored.** The existing eligibility rule refuses it, because no 45 deg set declares the passage-average friction. The f/f0 to friction conversion is registered as a definition, ready if that declaration is made.
  - **Broken and chevron ribs are not scored either.** No shipped set models them. Reprinted points from Rhee et al. (2003) and Wright et al. (2004) are committed but not scored.
  - **Fig. 18 is mis-computed in the paper.** The paper's own points are 3 (Nu/Nu0)/(f/f0), to 0.3%, not its Eq. 6. The reprinted points follow Eq. 6 to 0.13%. Tests pin both.
  - **Validation harness:** the Re_Dh rib path now scores a Nu ratio that is already on the Dittus-Boelter basis, and a friction ratio over Petukhov. It refuses a series whose axis name and declared convention disagree.
