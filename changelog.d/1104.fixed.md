- **`network_to_gui` exports effusion plates, film rows and slot films as their own nodes (#1104).** Every `OrificeElement` subclass used to be written as a plain `orifice` node, which lost the hole count, the hole Cd chain, the plate's wall, its gas side and its radiation, without saying so.
  - **Before:** a 69-hole effusion plate was written as one 8.31 mm orifice. The GUI solved it at Cd 0.645 instead of 0.864 and 24 % less coolant. A 20-hole film row came back at Cd 0.713 instead of 0.853 and 15.5 % less coolant. Inclined holes and every slot film were refused with the wrong reason ("orifice injection_angle_deg != 90").
  - **Now:** `EffusionPlateElement`, `FilmCoolingElement` and `SlotFilmElement` export to `effusion_plate`, `film_cooling` and `slot_film` nodes, and `NetworkRunner.from_dict(network_to_gui(net))` reproduces the script's flow, Cd and wall results. Each node carries every field of its form, its `hole_cd` and, on the plate, its radiation.
  - **Refused, by name:** a hole-Cd choice the node's Inspector does not offer, a hole `edge_radius`, a duct-fed plate (the GUI's duct-fed form is the effusion liner), a lone `ImpingementPlateElement` row, and the two connection elements. No `OrificeElement` subclass is written as an orifice.
