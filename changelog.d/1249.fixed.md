- **Wall-coupled channel partials (#1249, #1254, #1265, #1264).** Four Jacobian entries of wall-heated ducts now match finite differences.
  - **Fanno-Rayleigh flow (#1249):** the kernel's implicit derivatives use Richardson's extrapolation of its central differences, from a larger first step (4e-3 unchoked, 5e-4 choked) that the zeta check cuts where the march is nonlinear. The plain differences were 1.9e-6 off near an exit Mach of 0.8. Near the choke, where the step is cut below 1e-4, the plain difference is kept exactly as before. The flow itself is unchanged.
  - **Pin-fin surfaces (#1254):** `dh_dT` is now the array's own, through viscosity, conductivity and Pr. It used to be the smooth correlation's slope scaled to the pin-fin h, 21% low on a 300 K air array. `PinFinNuResult` gains `dNu_dPr`.
  - **One-stream mix at zero flow (#1265):** a mixer with a single stream keeps its partials inside the no-flow band. A wall-heated duct's own exit state is such a mix, and at exactly zero flow it dropped the exit Tt's 1:1 move with the duct's feed. That reversed the sign of the duct's flow row on an upstream flow. Two or more streams in the band still give zero partials (#1238).
  - **Ribbed surfaces (#1264):** `dh_dmdot` at exactly zero flow is the finite one-sided slope, not 0.
