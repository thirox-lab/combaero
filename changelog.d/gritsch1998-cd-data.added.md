- **New validation dataset: discharge coefficients of shaped film-cooling holes with crossflow.** `validation/cooling/data/gritsch1998/` holds Gritsch, Schulz and Wittig (1998), Figs. 3-14. It covers cylindrical, fan-shaped and laidback fan-shaped 30 deg holes (L/D 6), with coolant-side crossflow up to Ma_c 0.6 and mainstream crossflow up to Ma_m 1.2: 63 series and 500 digitised marks. Marks whose series was deduced from where the curves collapse together are filed in separate provisional series. Cd is referenced to the hole inlet (metering) area and to the same coolant-total-to-mainstream-static basis as Hay et al. (1983). The data are unscored, because combaero has no shaped-hole Cd. The extraction record `validation/cooling/extractions/gritsch_1998_shaped_hole_cd.md` states the gap: on these data, the plain-hole Cd that `FilmCoolingElement` applies to shaped holes under-predicts their flow by about 4-11 % without crossflow and 20-75 % with coolant crossflow.
