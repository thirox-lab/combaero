- `fanno_rayleigh_choked_alternative` reports whether choked flow is also admissible where a cooled Fanno-Rayleigh duct runs subsonic (#1307). Cooling raises a duct's Pt, so its needed drive can rise from zero flow to a peak and fall back; below that peak the duct is bistable. The jump to the choke as Tt_exit or L moves is a fold of the model (a 7.9x step in G at 1.5 Pa on a 1 cm duct from 400 K), so no root choice can remove it. The kernel keeps the start-up root, the state a slow ramp from rest reaches, now documented as the convention. A compressible `ChannelElement` (and `CrossflowSegmentElement`) carrying its heat in the momentum reports `bistable` and `m_dot_choked_alternative`, and a solve lists such ducts in `__bistable_ducts__`. Reported only: results are unchanged.
