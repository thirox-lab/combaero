- **A ThermalWall's k(T) loop no longer stops silently at its cap (#1170).** The layers' conductivity is iterated to its fixed point at each evaluation's wall temperatures (#481, #932).
  - **Before:** the loop stopped after 30 steps (60 with radiation) whether it had settled or not, and nothing said so. A metal on the hot side over alumina, with gas at 2000 K or more and coolant at 300 K or less, contracts by about 0.7 a step and reached the cap. Q then depended on the conductivity the previous evaluation left: up to 5e-8 relative, and 2e-5 with a 100 K coolant. No shipped network reached the cap.
  - **Now:** after 6 plain steps the loop takes Anderson steps (depth 2). On the issue's scans no call reaches the cap; the slowest needs 17 steps, and Q agrees across starts to 6e-13. A call that settles within 7 steps is computed exactly as before. The new `{wall}.kT_converged` result key is 1, or 0 when the report's own evaluation still ended at the cap.
  - **Known:** on a thick alumina stack at a cryogenic coolant, the layer-mean k(T) map can have several fixed points. There the loop can settle on a different root from the one the plain loop approached without reaching it (Q up to 5 % apart). The Inspector shows a note when `kT_converged` is 0.
