- **New validation dataset: liner air-entry hole discharge coefficient and jet angle under supply crossflow.** `validation/cooling/data/kaddah1964/` holds Kaddah (1964, Cranfield DAE thesis), Tables 11-30, as printed: 20 hole and slot configurations (circular, oval and rectangular, t/d 0.04-0.19, a* 0.06-0.6), 269 points at 0, 80 and 130 ft/s approach velocity. Each point gives C, K, the bleed ratio and the measured jet angle. The per-cell read confidence and seven printed misprints are listed in `cell_flags.csv`. `validation/cooling/kaddah_runner.py` scores McGreehan-Schotsch Eq. 17 and the full Cd chain against the data as cross-source accuracy. It reports U1/Vi under combaero's mean of the approaching and departing flow (the headline) and under Kaddah's approach velocity. On the liner-scale holes Eq. 17 is within a few percent at K >= 2 but reads 30-45% low at K < 2. The record is `validation/cooling/extractions/kaddah_1964_liner_hole_tables.md`.
