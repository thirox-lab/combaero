- **`EffusionPlateElement(crossflow_area=...)` may be omitted on a chain station.** A duct-fed panel (`crossflow_segments` given) whose supply node is a station of a `CrossflowSegmentElement` chain takes that station's section when `crossflow_area` is None; without a chain station the area is still required (now refused at validate, not at construction). Declared `crossflow_segments` or `crossflow_area` that contradict the station (other segments, or an area more than 1e-9 relative off) are refused at validate. `EffusionLiner`'s panels declare matching values and are unchanged.
