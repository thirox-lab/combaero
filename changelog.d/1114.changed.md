- **An unreachable fuel-controller target is refused before the solve where the inputs prove it (#1114).** A `FuelBoundary` target outside its reachable window used to be refused only after a full Newton search and its retries: 0.5-5.9 s (1.8 s mean) on the nine such draws of the `fuel_controller` stress family, seeds 0-99.
  - **Now:** when the combustor's inflows are fixed by the inputs -- every oxidizer source a fixed-flow `MassFlowBoundary`, reaching the combustor through plain orifices, lossless links and plenums with no wall or energy boundary, and the combustor with one outflow -- the combustor's Tt depends on the fuel/air ratio alone, and a target proven outside the window is refused in milliseconds with the same reason, prefixed "Refused before the solve". Proven: above the unsmoothed stoichiometric peak (lean, or rich with complete combustion), below the Tt with no injected fuel (lean), and rich with complete combustion below both the oxidizer's and the fuel's Tt.
  - **Unchanged:** air through a pressure boundary, a rich target on an equilibrium combustor, and a rich target below the oxidizer's Tt with fuel colder than the target, which has a fuel-as-coolant root at large phi (phi ~ 40); these still go through the solve. Converged solutions are bit-identical.
