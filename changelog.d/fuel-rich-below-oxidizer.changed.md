- **A rich fuel-controller target at or below the oxidizer's Tt is refused, never solved to a fuel-as-coolant root.** Below the oxidizer's Tt (what the combustor reaches with no injected fuel) but above the fuel's, a rich `FuelBoundary` target has a root at very rich phi, where the fuel cools the gas rather than burns: 700 K with air at 800 K and CH4 at 300 K converged at phi 44 (complete) or 39 (equilibrium) from a start near it, and was refused only when Newton missed it. The documented rule -- a target not above the oxidizer's Tt is refused -- now holds on the rich side as on the lean side, whatever the start.
  - Where the inputs fix the combustor's inflows (as #1114), refused before the solve, on either combustion method. Elsewhere (air through a pressure boundary) a converged rich root whose target is at or below the oxidizer's Tt at that root is refused with outcome `inconsistent`.
  - The reason now says the target is not above the oxidizer's Tt, so no combustion operating point exists, and that the non-reacting fuel-as-coolant solution at very rich phi is deliberately not returned.
