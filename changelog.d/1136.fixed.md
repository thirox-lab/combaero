- **A crossflow chain's exit into a `CombustorNode` is an exit at rest in both regimes (#1136).** A combustor holds Pt = P (`state_at_rest`), like a plenum, but the chain decided "at rest" by asking for a `PlenumNode` or a boundary. Compressible, the exit segment's total met the combustor's Pt and recovered its dynamic head; incompressible, it kept static continuity forward and, reversed, left out the acceleration out of the combustor that a plenum exit carries. The regimes differed by -21 % forward and +179 % reversed at 2 kPa on a one-station bleed chain.
  - **Now:** every reader of a chain's terminal end asks the node's own `state_at_rest` (the compressible terminal, the incompressible reversed exit and exit head, the start guess). A combustor exit solves a plenum exit's flow, regime gap +0.06 % forward and +0.96 % reversed at 2 kPa. The compressible forward flow into a combustor falls by 21 % (the head is lost by static continuity, the chain's convention at every node at rest) and the incompressible reversed flow by 64 %.
  - **Unchanged:** plenum, boundary and `MomentumChamberNode` exits, bit for bit in x, F and J. A `CombustorNode` station is still refused.
