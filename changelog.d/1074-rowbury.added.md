- **`EffusionPlateElement` and `FilmCoolingElement` take `cd_exit_crossflow="rowbury_2001"`, an opt-in exit (external) crossflow term in the discharge coefficient (#1074).** It applies Rowbury, Oldfield & Lock (2001) Eqs. 7-8 on top of the no-crossflow Cd: `1/Cd^2 = 1/Cd0^2 + A k^-B - C`.
  - **The momentum flux ratio `k`.** It is built from the hole's actual flow, the surface-normal share of the jet velocity (the paper's Fig. 3) and the coolant's isentropic exit-plane density. The mainstream is the approaching one: the main inlet of the discharge `MomentumChamberNode`.
  - **Coefficients and options.** `A`, `B` and `C` come from the paper's Table 2. `exit_crossflow_region` picks the set: `"suction_surface"` (the flat-wall default), `"pressure_surface"` or `"leading_edge"`. Shaped film holes take the fan-shaped sets. `exit_crossflow_orientation_deg` is the radial orientation alpha, measured from the surface normal. The term works with any correlation, including `"fixed"`, and with or without `cd_inclination`.
  - **Crossover.** It is built in inside the paper's fitted range (k up to 3.3): a strongly blowing hole's Cd rises. Beyond that range the `-C` offset fades. This is our extrapolation, and it makes the Cd return to the no-crossflow value continuously as the mainstream vanishes. With the leading-edge set at a no-crossflow Cd near 1, the Cd can exceed 1.
  - **Where it does not apply.** Without a chamber with an approaching mainstream, the opt-in is refused. Below a `k` floor the term is held. The defaults are unchanged bit for bit.
  - **Diagnostics, reported only when the term is on.** The actual and the evaluated `k`, the floor, the loss, and hold, fade and range flags.
  - **GUI.** The GUI backend reads the three fields from network JSON. There is no Inspector control yet.
- **New C++ `rowbury_2001_exit_crossflow_cd`, `rowbury_2001_coefficients`, `rowbury_2001_k_perp`, `rowbury_2001_exit_density`, `rowbury_2001_k_floor` and `rowbury_2001_in_range` (`crossflow_cd.h`).** All derivatives are analytic.
- **`validation/cooling/exit_crossflow_cd_runner.py` reports fidelity and cross-source accuracy for the term.**
  - **Fidelity.** Against the authors' own Fig. 9 predictions, read by eye, the term is within 0.003 at Ma_m 0.6 and 1.2.
  - **Accuracy, cross-source, with the inclination term on.** On Gritsch 1998 the mean error moves from +0.8% to -2.8% at Ma_m 0.3, from +15.9% to +3.2% at Ma_m 0.6, and from +46.8% to +8.6% at Ma_m 1.2. On Hay 1983 at Minf 0.31 it moves from +11.4% to +6.8% and from +12.3% to +8.8% on the 30 deg holes. The 90 deg holes go from +11.2% to -4.7%. They lie outside the suction-surface set's fitted 30-78 deg, and that result depends on the floor's margin, which is combaero's own choice: between -0.3% and -9.4%.
  - **Behrendt 2008.** On the 30 deg plates the term raises the coolant flow by 0.9-1.3%. That is the wrong direction for the rig's coolant-flow excess, and it is reported, not tuned.
