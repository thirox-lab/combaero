- **A heated or cooled channel's flow no longer depends on the solver's history near the choke (#1161).** `fanno_rayleigh_channel_flow` called without a warm start (`G_guess <= 0`) scans the flux up in factors of 2 toward the choked flux Gc. The march goes sonic about 3e-7 below Gc, so it read the last bracket's upper end as choked, and the scan gave up on that bracket without testing inside it. Every root above about 0.93 Gc was missed. The kernel then returned `G = Gc` with `choked = False`, `M_exit = 1` and a nonzero `dG_dP_target` on a flux that was flat in the drive. A warm start (`G_guess` = the element's last flux) found the real root. So the residual of a `ChannelElement` on a `ThermalWall` depended on whether the element had been evaluated before, and on the flux it had then. On the stress `hotcold` draws 10, 11, 12, 16, 18 and 26, a fresh solver's residual at the solved root differed from the solved solver's by 3-7 % of the flow row's scale. No converged stress result stood on the plateau: each solve's last evaluation was warm-started onto the real root, but on 14 of the 40 `hotcold` roots a cold call gave the plateau instead (up to 6.1 % in the flux).
  - **Now:** when the march reads a bracket's upper end as choked, the scan cuts it back to the largest flux the march still carries subsonically and tests for the fall there. Cold and warm calls give the same root, to within 1e-11 relative. On those six draws the solved and fresh residuals agree to 2e-13 of the row scale, and on all 640 stress draws (seeds 0:40) every kernel call at the root gives the same flux cold and warm. The stress outcomes are unchanged; the three draws that flipped in one serial run each (`effusion` 33, `random` 18, `jet_chain` 1) never call this kernel and converged in the repeat.
  - **Derivatives near the choke:** the roots now reached include those within ~1e-4 of Gc, where the kernel's 1e-4 difference steps crossed the march's sonic point and put 4-96 % errors into every derivative (a warm start could reach some of them before). Those steps are now cut until they move the march's zeta by at most 2 % of itself. Where the full step already stays within that, the derivatives are unchanged bit for bit. Measured against central differences of the kernel's own flux, from 30 % of the choking drive up to 99.9 % of it (8 heated and cooled ducts, total- and static-pressure exits), the scaled error is at most 2.4e-5 with the cut steps and up to 0.96 without. One point read 3.1e-4: the reference difference's own step, which converges onto the kernel's value (to 1e-6) as it shrinks.
  - **Static-pressure exit:** fluxes within ~1e-10 of the march's sonic flux are no longer treated as carried subsonically. A root there gave an infinite `dG_dP_target`. The drive band they span, about 2 Pa just below the choke, is now reported choked at Gc; the flux there was within 3e-7 of Gc either way.
  - **Unchanged:** every call whose root the old scan found, and every choked call, gives the same flux and derivatives bit for bit, except where the last bracket's upper end lay within the new 1e-10 zeta floor (the flux moves by ~1e-14 relative).
