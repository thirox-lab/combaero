- **A closed-end or to-only crossflow station reports its moving state.** Row 1 of an impingement gap (a `CrossflowSegmentElement` from-station with nothing arriving) reported `.T = .Tt`, `.Pt = .P` and `.mach = 0`, though its gas moves at half the leaving segment's flow; so did a to-station (`to_kappa` with `next_seg`) whose next segment declares no `from_kappa`, though its gas moves at the two segments' mean flow (a duct-fed panel there: Mach 0 -> 0.34). Both now report the static T, true Pt and Mach at that mean flow, the state the residuals already used, and the end segments' `Pt_in`/`Pt_out`, `T_in`/`T_out` (and the `dPt`, `pr_total` built from them) follow. No residual, Jacobian or solution changes. The chain's second-law check reads the station's reported state, so its `sigma_norm` (with a heated station, also `sigma_norm_low`/`_high`) moves at the 1e-5 relative level.
  - Every reader of a station's moving state -- the result report and a duct-fed `EffusionPlateElement`'s supply drive, `U1/Vi` and Cd conversion -- now reads one station description the chain registers on the node at topology, instead of computing it in three places.
  - A node wired as the station of two different segment pairs (two segments leaving it with `from_kappa`, or a from-station beside a to-only one) is refused at validate, naming both; before, the last one wired silently won.
