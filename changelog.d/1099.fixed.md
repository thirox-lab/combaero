- **`FlowNetwork.from_dict(net.to_dict())` rebuilds the network exactly (#1099).** `to_dict` reads each constructor argument back from the attribute of the same name, and several classes rewrite that attribute in `resolve_topology` or keep the argument under another name.
  - **Before:** every network with an impingement gap failed to load (`ImpingementCrossflowElement` got its resolved `entry_K=None`). Inherited geometry was saved as if the user had given it: a momentum chamber's or combustor's area as its `0.0` placeholder before a solve, or frozen at the inherited value after one, and likewise a channel's diameter and Dh, a pressure loss's area, a tee's `F_C`/`F_branch`, a junction's port areas and an ejector's mixing area. An orifice's selected compressible model was saved as explicit. A junction's port angles came back as zero. `EffectiveAreaConnectionElement`, the junction elements and the ejector could not be loaded at all, and energy boundaries were dropped.
  - **Now:** each class names the attribute that holds its argument, energy boundaries are saved with their node, and `from_dict` finds the classes outside `components`. The reloaded network's residuals and Jacobian are bit-identical over every stress family.
  - The GUI saves its own schema and is unaffected.
