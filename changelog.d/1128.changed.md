- Changed `PressureLossElement` (when it has a velocity area), `AreaChangeElement` (normal and pass-through), `LosslessConnectionElement` (when it has an area or leaves a `MomentumChamberNode`), and pass-through `OrificeElement` (`bore == D_pipe` or `bore == D_chamber`) to honour `PressureBoundary.exit_head_lost` at outflow boundaries (#1128). Under the default `coupling="auto"` (and `"static"`), these elements now discharge to the boundary static pressure and lose their exit dynamic head (`exit_head_recovered == 0`), matching `ChannelElement` and `PortDischargeElement` (for example, on Probe S4 -- `PB 1.05 bar -> 0.5 m x 50 mm duct -> chamber -> zero-loss element -> PB 1.00 bar` -- mass flow moves from `0.42333 kg/s` with recovered head to `0.19562 kg/s`, matching a short duct; `LosslessConnectionElement` leaving a `PlenumNode` with no area is unchanged). Set `PressureBoundary(..., coupling="total")` to impose stagnation pressure and retain the previous total-to-total recovery behaviour.
