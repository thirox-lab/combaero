- **A crossflow segment refuses a negative or non-finite `entry_K` (#1220).** A negative entry loss adds total pressure, which breaks the second law. `CrossflowSegmentElement` used to accept one, and so did `EffusionLiner.entry_K`, which feeds it.
  - **The effect:** K = -0.5 raised the flow by about 35 %. K = -1 divided by zero in the compressible entry term.
  - **Now:** `CrossflowSegmentElement` raises `ValueError` and names the segment; every subclass and builder goes through that one check. `ImpingementCrossflowElement` and `ImpingementArray` already refused a negative value.
