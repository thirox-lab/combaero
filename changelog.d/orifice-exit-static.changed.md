- **A plain orifice or a Cd/effective-area connection into a flowing chamber blows until its supply meets the chamber's static pressure (#1087).** `OrificeElement`, `EffectiveAreaConnectionElement` and `DiameterDischargeCoefficientConnectionElement` now drive as the film, effusion and slot holes do.
  - **Before.** Into a `MomentumChamberNode` the direction came from `Pt_from - Pt_to`, so the element reversed wherever `P_to < Pt_from < Pt_to`, although its jet discharges against the static. Across `Pt_to` the flow jumped by most of a jet inside the 3 Pa blend.
  - **Now.** The direction comes from `Pt_from - P_to`. A forward flow discharges against the chamber's static. A reversed flow draws from that static and discharges against `Pt_from`. The flow passes zero once, continuously, and the Jacobian stays analytic through the switch. The second-law control volume reads the same pair of pressures.
  - **An approach pipe.** Forward the drive reads the approach pipe's static as before. Reversed, the flow draws from the chamber static as the approach static, with no #651 correction on top. A chamber in line with its single pipe (nothing else at it) carries the orifice's own flow, so the velocity of approach (`beta_reverse`, its pipe Cd) is kept and the flow equals the plenum version's. A side-injection chamber has no approach toward the bore, so its reversed leg applies none; it would count the pipe's head twice. A side-injection hole into a duct-fed chamber therefore behaves the same whatever the chamber's outlet element. `dP_tap` and the compressible-model diagnostics of a reversed jet read the kernel's pair (the chamber static minus the supply `Pt`, not the supply node's own static), for the film, effusion and slot holes too.
  - **Unchanged.** A recovering in-pipe metering orifice (ISO permanent loss, #645), a pass-through bore and a closed one keep the `Pt - Pt` direction; their residuals and Jacobians are bit-identical to before. Every element into a plenum or a pressure boundary is unchanged. `ImpingementPlateElement` follows later.
