- **`HoleCd(supply_drive=...)`: the pressure a duct-fed hole drives from (#1087, supply side).** A duct-fed `EffusionPlateElement` (each `EffusionLiner` panel) has a duct station as its supply node, and the station holds the duct's static pressure.
  - **The choices.** `"static"` (`"auto"`, unchanged) drives from that station static, McGreehan & Schotsch's own convention. `"total"` drives from the duct's stagnation pressure at the station: the static plus the dynamic head of the mean segment flow, from C++ `hole_supply_drive` (the momentum-chamber closure, analytic partials). `"static"` on a hole that is not duct-fed is refused.
  - **`"total"` requires `inlet_crossflow="none"`.** It is refused with McGreehan-Schotsch's Eq. 17 term on. That Cd is referenced to the station static, so on the duct total it is not the authors' model, and converted exactly it gives the static drive's flow again.
  - **Choosing a drive is choosing the recovered fraction f of the supply's dynamic head** (f = 0 static, f = 1 total). The held data split by lean (cross-source). f ~ 0 holds for normal holes (4 labs, 11 sets) and for holes leaning across the supply flow (Hay 1983). f ~ 1 holds for 30 deg holes leaning with it (Gritsch 1998, one lab). Static is therefore the default, and co-aligned holes are its known miss. The route to a lean-dependent f is a second co-aligned lab (Rohde 1969 Fig. 11, held) and Gritsch, Schulz & Wittig 2001 (J. Turbomach. 123(4), 781-787, doi:10.1115/1.1397306).
  - **Diagnostics.** The `Cd_*` group gains `Cd_supply_drive_model` (text), `Cd_supply_drive_applied` and `Cd_supply_drive_head_fraction`. The last is information: the duct's dynamic head over the full drive, the share f decides.
  - **Known limitations.** The static drive takes the station's total temperature, where McGreehan & Schotsch take the static temperature (0 to +3.5 % in the ideal flow on Gritsch's points). With `"total"`, a duct-fed panel's jet momentum into a merge chamber still takes the station's own pressure.
