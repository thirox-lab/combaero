- **Equilibrium combustion converges for heptane with a trace of air (#1287).** Heptane with air at a mole fraction of 3e-21 to 1e-12 was refused: 4 to 10 of 10 states at each trace level from 1e-20 to 1e-16 (250-900 K, 2 and 20 bar), and some at every level up to 1e-12. Now every state from 1e-20 up converges at 1 to 200 bar (from 1e-19 up at 0.01 and 0.1 bar). T joins the zero-air T linearly. In a 24,882-state draw over seven fuels, and in an independent 15,500-state draw, states that converged before move by about 1e-11 K at most.
  - **Element-potential start in a component basis:** with heptane the only major species, it dominated every element row, and the traces' share, which sets their distribution, fell below the rows' rounding. The start never converged, and its steam came out 1e10 times the oxygen total. The element equations are now written in a basis of component species: the most abundant independent ones, as Smith and Missen's stoichiometric formulation. A major species then contributes exactly nothing to a trace component's row.
  - **Subnormal amounts:** at 1e-20 of air, steam's equilibrium amount is subnormal (4e-315). Its J_aa overflowed to inf and the Newton system to NaN; its scale is now taken in a finite form, in the Newton step and in the Jacobian. Its residual floor now counts the fewer digits a subnormal amount holds.
  - **Heptane at zero air:** its Jacobian is now checked an instant later. With air at 1e-18 and 1e-24 kg/s it equals the zero-air J to 1e-12.
  - **Other fuels with a vanishing trace of air:** methane, ethane, propane, isobutane, pentane and hexane with air at 1e-30 to 1e-100 were refused in 39 to 60 of 60 states each (250-900 K, 0.01-20 bar). Now every state converges.
  - **Still refused:** heptane with air below about 1e-20, where its steam lies below the smallest double. That is about 3e-21 at 1 bar and above; the bound rises as the pressure falls (#1290).
