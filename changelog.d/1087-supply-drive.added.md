- **Every hole drives from the upstream total to the downstream static; `HoleCd(supply_drive=..., supply_recovery=...)` (#1087, supply side).** A duct-fed `EffusionPlateElement` (each `EffusionLiner` panel) has a duct station as its supply node, and the station holds the duct's static pressure.
  - **The drive.** `"auto"` is the duct's stagnation pressure at the station: the static plus the dynamic head of the mean segment flow, from C++ `hole_supply_drive` (the momentum-chamber closure, analytic partials, smooth through a duct-flow reversal). The rule makes the element own the energy balance. How much of the supply head q1 = Pt1 - P1 the hole recovers is its Cd model's business: the recovered fraction f, `HoleCd.supply_recovery`. Design record: `docs/archive/hole_drive_convention.md`.
  - **Every base Cd is referenced to P1 + f q1 and converted exactly onto the total drive** with C++ `hole_supply_recovery_cd_ratio`: Cd_T = Cd_base m_id(P1 + f q1, Tf -> p2) / m_id(Pt1, Tt1 -> p2), compressible, from the network's own nozzle kernel, with T1 the station's static temperature and Tf the isentropic T at P1 + f q1 (analytic partials, d/df included). The hole passes zero at P1 + f q1. The conversion applies at a `MomentumChamberNode` station too (its static P and static T). An element in the incompressible regime, and the solver's incompressible warm-start proxy, take the incompressible kernel's exact ratio sqrt(1 - (1 - f) X), X = q1/(Pt1 - p2). `hole_static_to_total_cd_ratio` is the f = 0 case, bit for bit.
  - **f = 0 by default, at every angle and for every base (owner, 2026-11-13).** Held normal-hole Cd data (Kaddah 1964, Rohde TN D-5467, McGreehan & Schotsch Fig. 6, Hay, Lampard & Benmansour 1983: 4 labs, 11 sets) give f ~ 0 (within-set RMS 15.8 % on the static basis against 32.5 % on the total, cross-source). For McGreehan-Schotsch's Eq. 17 term, the liner default, it is the authors' own model: their Cd is referenced to the station static pressure and temperature. Liner flows equal the v0.11.1 ones up to the static-temperature correction, which grows with the station Mach (+1 to +3 % on the probe liners, +3.6 % at a high-Mach single station, up to +5.4 % on 12-station liners at high duct Mach), with the term on or off (`inlet_crossflow="none"`: +1.6 % in total panel flow on the 3-segment test liner, -0.19 / +1.80 / +2.43 % per panel), and the reported `Cd` / `Cd_base` are on the total basis.
  - **f applies to a base Cd defined plenum-fed (`inlet_crossflow="none"`).** Where the correlation owns the supply crossflow (McGreehan-Schotsch with its U1/Vi term on, the liner default) any `supply_recovery`, 0.0 included, is refused: a recovery on top of Eq. 17 counts the crossflow twice. `supply_recovery=1.0` recovers the full head, the plain Cd from the total to the static; on a 3-segment liner with the term off it bleeds 2.3x the default's flow, and a reversed jet discharges against the duct total. Which holes recover how much is #1124. A value in between is the linear share of the head. f is also refused outside [0, 1], with `supply_drive="static"`, and on every other hole element (film, slot, impingement row, plain orifice, a plenum-fed plate). Route to another default: the lean- and direction-dependent recovery, #1124.
  - **`"static"` breaks the Pt-to-static rule.** It is the v0.11.1 drive exactly (station static, node total temperature, the base Cd unconverted) and exists only to reproduce v0.11.1 liner results. It will be removed once the lean/recovery term (#1124) is settled by Gritsch 2001 or Rohde data. It is refused on a hole that is not duct-fed.
  - **GUI.** `HoleCdData.supply_drive` (`"auto"` / `"static"`, default `"auto"`) and `HoleCdData.supply_recovery` (default None) are carried through files and the graph builder. A file without the keys (1.3, or a lifted v0.11.1 one) loads as `"auto"` and f = 0. The effusion liner's Hole Cd editor takes f ("Supply Head Recovered") while the supply-crossflow term is None; `supply_drive` has no editor select (#1124).
  - **Diagnostics.** The `Cd_*` group gains `Cd_supply_drive_model` (text: `"recovery"`, the `"auto"` drive, or `"static"`), `Cd_supply_drive_applied`, `Cd_supply_drive_head_fraction` (information: the duct's dynamic head over the full drive), `Cd_supply_recovery_q1` (the head), `Cd_supply_recovery_fraction` (the f applied; the lean recovery is #1124) and `Cd_supply_recovery_ratio` (Cd_T / Cd_base).
  - **Known gaps.** Held data (cross-source) give f ~ 0 for normal holes and holes leaning across the supply flow, and f ~ 1 for 30 deg holes leaning with it (one lab). So the default f = 0 misses co-aligned holes, which take `supply_recovery=1.0` until #1124. The lean- and direction-dependent recovery is a Cd-model term to come (#1124), from Rohde 1969 Fig. 11 and Gritsch, Schulz & Wittig 2001 (J. Turbomach. 123(4), 781-787, doi:10.1115/1.1397306). A liner whose duct flow reverses at a station (fed from both ends, or back-pressured) is demoted by the second-law check, because the station's reversed-flow model manufactures total pressure (#1125); some such liners converged on 0.11.x. When the solver's warm-start proxy root is refused for it, the failure message names the element and its `sigma_norm` and points at #1125. Cold-start convergence of 12-station liners with `inlet_crossflow="none"` at f = 1 drops against 0.11.x (61 -> 52 and 46 -> 30 of 64 on a serial sweep), and one `HoleCd()` case of 128 is lost (#1130).
