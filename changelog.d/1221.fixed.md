- **A cooled channel's warm call no longer takes a fold's rising crossing when its bracket ends inside the march's noise (#1221).** A cooled duct's exit-pressure excess can fall through zero, dip and rise back; at the dip's rising crossing it is a root too, and within the march's rounding noise of it its sign is noise. `fanno_rayleigh_channel_flow` accepted a warm bracket ending there as a sign change, and Brent converged on a noise crossing next to that end: the rising crossing, 1.001-1.054x the first fall (the dip's width), while the cold call returned the first fall. The answer depended on the solver's previous flux.
  - **Now:** on a cooled duct a warm bracket is taken only where the excess at both ends is clear of `kRayleighExcessNoise` = 1e-13 Pt0; otherwise the call goes the cold way. The constant is measured, not fitted to the misses: the march's scatter is at most 7e-14 Pt0 plus 6.2e-14 of the excess's slope in log G (7749 samples, two duct generators, 1e-4 Gc to 3e-8 below the choke). 3e-14 and 1e-10 Pt0 fix the same misses on the design sweeps below; 1e-10 also declined brackets whose ends sat 1e4x above the noise.
  - **Measured** against a dense-grid first fall (grid plus brentq on the march, no kernel bracket logic), on dips deeper than 100x the march's noise floor: the #1206 fold sweep, warm misses 5 -> 0 of 5785; warm brackets placed with an end exactly on a fold's crossings, misses 158 -> 0 of 34.6k warm calls and near the choke 633 -> 0 of 20.7k; held out (fresh rsweep seeds and the #1225 review's generator, 5000 ducts), fold sweeps 5 -> 0, placed ends 310 -> 0 and near the choke 971 -> 0, warm != cold 1457 -> 0. Every changed result there is the cold one, and each that moved by more than 1e-5 moved down, to the first fall. On dips shallower than that the first fall is itself a noise reading; the warm call now returns the cold one there too.
  - **Realistic sweeps** (rsweep seeds 31 and 32, 2500 ducts, 101k calls with every friction model, guesses around each crossing): no result changed, in any field.
  - **Unchanged:** heated and adiabatic ducts (bit-identical, every output field), the cold scan, and every cooled warm call whose bracket ends are clear of the noise.
