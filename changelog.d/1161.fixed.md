- **A heated or cooled channel's flow no longer depends on the solver's history near the choke (#1161).** `fanno_rayleigh_channel_flow` called without a warm start (`G_guess <= 0`) scans the flux up in factors of 2 toward the choked flux Gc. The march goes sonic about 3e-7 below Gc, so it read the last bracket's upper end as choked, and the scan gave up on that bracket without testing inside it. Every root above about 0.93 Gc was missed. The kernel then returned `G = Gc` with `choked = False`, `M_exit = 1` and a nonzero `dG_dP_target` on a flux that was flat in the drive. A warm start (`G_guess` = the element's last flux) found the real root. So the residual of a `ChannelElement` on a `ThermalWall` depended on whether the element had been evaluated before, and on the flux it had then. On the stress `hotcold` draws 10, 11, 12, 16, 18 and 26, a fresh solver's residual at the solved root differed from the solved solver's by 3-7 % of the flow row's scale. No converged stress result stood on the plateau: each solve's last evaluation was warm-started onto the real root, but on 14 of the 40 `hotcold` roots a cold call gave the plateau instead (up to 6.1 % in the flux).
  - **Now:** when the march reads a bracket's upper end as choked, the scan cuts it back to the largest flux the march still carries subsonically and tests for the fall there. Cold and warm calls now give the same root. They agree to ~1e-8 relative at 1-3 Pa of drive on 1 MPa, where the excess is flat in the flux and Brent's tolerance shows (the same on main), and to ~1e-11 from a few kPa up. On those six draws the solved and fresh residuals agree to 2e-13 of the row scale. On all 640 stress draws (seeds 0:40), every kernel call at the root gives the same flux cold and warm, to 1e-10. The stress outcomes are unchanged; the three draws that flipped in one serial run each (`effusion` 33, `random` 18, `jet_chain` 1) never call this kernel and converged in the repeat.
  - **Still history-dependent (as on main, not fixed here):** a cooled duct whose excess falls through zero and rises above it again inside one ln-2 bracket of the scan. The drive it needs peaks and turns back down. A cold call sees both bracket ends positive and reports it choked at Gc, while a warm start finds the first crossing unchoked. Two examples on a static exit: a 3 MPa, 1400 -> 1000 K, 0.03 m duct at drives of 259963-261953 Pa (a warm start finds 0.975 Gc); a 2 bar, 1800 -> 400 K duct at drives of about 98-108 Pa (a warm start finds 0.12 Gc). No stress draw meets it.
  - **Derivatives near the choke:** the roots now reached include those within ~1e-4 of Gc, where the kernel's 1e-4 difference steps crossed the march's sonic point and put 4-96 % errors into every derivative (a warm start could reach some of them before). Those steps are now cut until they move the march's zeta by at most 2 % of itself. Measured against central differences of the kernel's own flux, from 30 % of the choking drive up to 99.9 % of it (8 heated and cooled ducts, total- and static-pressure exits), the scaled error is at most 2.4e-5 with the cut steps and up to 0.96 without. One point read 3.1e-4: the reference difference's own step, which converges onto the kernel's value (to 1e-6) as it shrinks. Closer to the choke the error grows. Within 1 Pa of the choking drive it is up to 5e-5 on heated total-pressure exits and 1e-3 on the cooled hotcold-10 duct. Within about 10 Pa of a static exit's choke it is up to 1e-2 (8e-3 at 10 Pa and 9e-3 at 1 Pa).
  - **Choke edge:** a flux whose march reaches `zeta_min <= 1e-10` (within ~1e-10 of the march's own sonic flux) no longer counts as carried subsonically. A root there gave an infinite `dG_dP_target`. On static-pressure exits the drives this excludes span 0.1-8 Pa just below the choke, about 1e-6 to 5e-6 of the choking drive; they are now reported choked at Gc. On total-pressure exits the band is at most 1e-5 Pa. The flux there is within 3e-7 of Gc either way. A static duct that chokes at its inlet (the march is subsonic at Gc) keeps a 0.003-0.04 Pa band where G == Gc comes back unchoked, as on main; there G is right to 1e-12.
  - **Unchanged:** every call whose root the old scan found, and every choked call, gives the same flux, except where the last bracket's upper end lay within the new zeta floor (the flux then moves by ~1e-14 relative). The derivatives are bit-identical only where the full difference step already moved zeta by no more than 2 %. Closer to the choke, roots the old scan or a warm start found also get new derivatives. Example: stress `impingement` seed 3, a short near-adiabatic duct at 0.999 Gc, where `dG_dP_target` moves from -0.030605 to -0.030630 (the converged difference is -0.030630). So the Jacobian moves in the impingement family too, not only in `hotcold`.
