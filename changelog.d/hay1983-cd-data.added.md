- **New validation dataset: hole discharge coefficient against hole angle and two-sided crossflow.** `validation/cooling/data/hay1983/` holds Hay, Lampard and Benmansour (1983), Figs. 5, 6, 8, 11, 12 and 15: 25 series, 174 digitised marks of Cd for 30 and 90 deg holes (L/D 6), with coolant-side and mainstream-side crossflow varied independently and the coolant duct parallel or perpendicular to the mainstream. The data are unscored, because no combaero closure has an angle or crossflow-direction term. They are cross-source accuracy data for a future one. The Cd basis, coolant stagnation to mainstream static, is declared in `conventions.py`. The extraction record is `validation/cooling/extractions/hay_1983_crossflow_cd.md`, which also holds the printed plateau values of Figs. 9, 10, 13 and 14 and two suspected misprints.
