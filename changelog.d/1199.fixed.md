- **A round-off composition component no longer switches a duct's composition derivative to one-sided (#1199).** A duct differences its flux along the composition directions its feeding node moves in, central unless a step would take a mass fraction below zero.
  - **Before:** a direction component at round-off level (2.2e-20) on a species absent at the node decided the stencil by its sign and switched it to a first-order one-sided difference, O(h) with h = 1e-5. In the stress sweep this fired at 46 of the 510 directional differences taken inside the duct kernels during Jacobian evaluations, at 8 of 1908 points, and moved the Jacobian by up to 5e-7 scaled (2.3e-5 relative on stress random 30's `J[e34, e9.m_dot]`).
  - **Now:** a component at or below 1e-12 of the direction's largest that would take its fraction below zero is dropped from the step, and those derivatives are central again; they agree with a network finite difference to 1e-10 scaled. A genuine one-sided case (a species absent or at trace level, with a real component) uses a second-order stencil, at half the step where a second trace species blocks the doubled one: 3e-6 to 1e-5 relative error before, about 5e-8 now, with no jump as a trace fraction crosses 2h. F is unchanged everywhere, and J is bit-identical at the other 1900 stress points.
