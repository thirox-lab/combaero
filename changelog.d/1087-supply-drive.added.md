- **Every hole drives from the upstream total to the downstream static; `HoleCd(supply_drive=...)` (#1087, supply side).** A duct-fed `EffusionPlateElement` (each `EffusionLiner` panel) has a duct station as its supply node, and the station holds the duct's static pressure.
  - **The drive.** `"total"` (`"auto"`) is the duct's stagnation pressure at the station: the static plus the dynamic head of the mean segment flow, from C++ `hole_supply_drive` (the momentum-chamber closure, analytic partials, smooth through a duct-flow reversal). The rule makes the element own the energy balance. How much of the supply head the hole recovers (the recovered fraction f) is its Cd model's business. Design record: `docs/archive/hole_drive_convention.md`.
  - **McGreehan-Schotsch's Eq. 17 term, converted exactly.** It is the liner default, and its Cd is referenced to the station static pressure and temperature, so it is carried onto the total drive with C++ `hole_static_to_total_cd_ratio`: Cd_T = Cd_S m_id(p1, T1 -> p2) / m_id(Pt1, Tt1 -> p2), compressible, from the network's own nozzle kernel. That is the authors' own model (f = 0). Liner flows equal the pre-0.12 ones up to the static-temperature correction (+1 to +3 % on the probe liners), and the reported `Cd` / `Cd_base` are on the total basis.
  - **`inlet_crossflow="none"` is f = 1:** the plain Cd from the total to the static, so the full supply head is recovered. That is the behaviour of a hole leaning with the supply flow. On a liner with the term off it bleeds **+70 to +250 %** more than the pre-0.12 static drive did.
  - **`"static"`** is the pre-0.12 drive (station static, node total temperature), kept to reproduce earlier results. It is refused on a hole that is not duct-fed.
  - **Diagnostics.** The `Cd_*` group gains `Cd_supply_drive_model` (text), `Cd_supply_drive_applied` and `Cd_supply_drive_head_fraction` (information: the duct's dynamic head over the full drive).
  - **Known gaps.** Held data (cross-source) give f ~ 0 for normal holes and holes leaning across the supply flow, and f ~ 1 for 30 deg holes leaning with it (one lab). So Eq. 17 misses co-aligned holes and a term-off base misses normal ones. The lean- and direction-dependent recovery is a Cd-model term to come (#1124), from Rohde 1969 Fig. 11 and Gritsch, Schulz & Wittig 2001 (J. Turbomach. 123(4), 781-787, doi:10.1115/1.1397306). A liner whose duct flow reverses at a station is demoted by the second-law check, because the station's reversed-flow model manufactures total pressure (#1125).
