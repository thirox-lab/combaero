- **The combustor kernel's pressure loss sees the flow it burns (#1239).** `combustor_residuals_and_jacobians` handed its pressure-loss callable `mdot_total` as the signed sum of all streams.
  - **The problem:** beside a stream leaving the combustor, that sum shrank toward zero or changed sign while gas still arrived. 0.104 kg/s arriving beside 0.103 kg/s leaving gave 0.001 kg/s.
  - **Now:** `mdot_total` is the total of the arriving streams. The kernel already mixes those streams, burns them and spreads its heat over them (#1232). With no stream arriving it is the sum of all streams, as the kernel's reverse mean (#1224).
  - **Networks are unchanged.** The solver points every stream it hands a node by the sign of its flow (#481, #517, #1231), so a node takes no leaving stream. The two totals then agree, and so do a node's throughput and the flow arriving at it. The kernel's sum is bit-identical wherever its streams share a sign.
    - **Tested:** 2626 points of the Jacobian sweep, through a reversed two-port element and a reversed junction port, find no leaving stream at any plenum, chamber, combustor, wall or mass-flow node.
    - **Known exception:** a junction port fed by a legacy tee leg has no flow index to reverse by. It can take a leaving stream, and its mass balance does not close even without one (#1301).
  - **Who sees it:** a direct caller of the kernel that passes streams of both signs and a loss callable that reads `mdot_total`. The network's combustor passes a zero-loss callable.
