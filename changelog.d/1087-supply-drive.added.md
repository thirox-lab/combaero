- **Every hole drives from the upstream total to the downstream static; `HoleCd(supply_drive=...)` (#1087, supply side).** A duct-fed `EffusionPlateElement` (each `EffusionLiner` panel) has a duct station as its supply node, and the station holds the duct's static pressure.
  - **The drive.** `"total"` (`"auto"`) is the duct's stagnation pressure at the station: the static plus the dynamic head of the mean segment flow, from C++ `hole_supply_drive` (the momentum-chamber closure, analytic partials, smooth through a duct-flow reversal). The rule makes the element own the energy balance. How much of the supply head the hole recovers (the recovered fraction f) is its Cd model's business. Design record: `docs/archive/hole_drive_convention.md`.
  - **McGreehan-Schotsch's Eq. 17 term, converted exactly.** It is the liner default, and its Cd is referenced to the station static pressure and temperature, so it is carried onto the total drive with C++ `hole_static_to_total_cd_ratio`: Cd_T = Cd_S m_id(p1, T1 -> p2) / m_id(Pt1, Tt1 -> p2), compressible, from the network's own nozzle kernel. That is the authors' own model (f = 0). Liner flows equal the pre-0.12 ones up to the static-temperature correction, which grows with the station Mach (+1 to +3 % on the probe liners, +3.6 % at a high-Mach single station, up to +5.4 % on 12-station liners at high duct Mach), and the reported `Cd` / `Cd_base` are on the total basis. The conversion applies at a `MomentumChamberNode` station too (its static P and static T). An element in the incompressible regime, and the solver's incompressible warm-start proxy, take the incompressible kernel's exact ratio sqrt(1 - X), X = q1/(Pt1 - p2) (`hole_static_to_total_cd_ratio(..., incompressible=True)`).
  - **`inlet_crossflow="none"` is f = 1:** the plain Cd from the total to the static, so the full supply head is recovered. That is the behaviour of a hole leaning with the supply flow. On a liner with the term off it bleeds **+70 to +250 %** (from +4 % at a low duct Mach) more than the pre-0.12 static drive did, and a reversed jet discharges against the duct total.
  - **`"static"` breaks the Pt-to-static rule.** It is the pre-0.12 drive (station static, node total temperature) and exists only to reproduce v0.11.1 liner results. It will be removed once the lean/recovery term (#1124) is settled by Gritsch 2001 or Rohde data. It is refused on a hole that is not duct-fed.
  - **GUI.** `HoleCdData.supply_drive` (default `"auto"`) is carried through files and the graph builder. A file without the key (1.3, or a lifted v0.11.1 one) loads as `"auto"`. There is no editor select yet (#1124).
  - **Diagnostics.** The `Cd_*` group gains `Cd_supply_drive_model` (text), `Cd_supply_drive_applied` and `Cd_supply_drive_head_fraction` (information: the duct's dynamic head over the full drive).
  - **Known gaps.** Held data (cross-source) give f ~ 0 for normal holes and holes leaning across the supply flow, and f ~ 1 for 30 deg holes leaning with it (one lab). So Eq. 17 misses co-aligned holes and a term-off base misses normal ones. The lean- and direction-dependent recovery is a Cd-model term to come (#1124), from Rohde 1969 Fig. 11 and Gritsch, Schulz & Wittig 2001 (J. Turbomach. 123(4), 781-787, doi:10.1115/1.1397306). A liner whose duct flow reverses at a station (fed from both ends, or back-pressured) is demoted by the second-law check, because the station's reversed-flow model manufactures total pressure (#1125); some such liners converged on 0.11.x. When the solver's warm-start proxy root is refused for it, the failure message names the element and its `sigma_norm` and points at #1125. Cold-start convergence of 12-station liners with `inlet_crossflow="none"` drops against 0.11.x (61 -> 52 and 46 -> 30 of 64 on a serial sweep), and one `HoleCd()` case of 128 is lost (#1130).
