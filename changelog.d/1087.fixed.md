- **A film, effusion or slot hole into a flowing chamber blows until its coolant meets the chamber's static pressure (#1087).** This changes the residual of every such hole near a `MomentumChamberNode`'s dynamic-head band.
  - **Before.** The direction came from coolant Pt against chamber Pt, so the hole ingested wherever `P_static,chamber < Pt_coolant < Pt_chamber`, although its jet discharges against the static.
  - **Now.** The direction comes from the hole's drive against the chamber's static pressure, and a reversed jet draws from that static. None of the chamber's dynamic head is taken as recovered, since no source gives the fraction. The reversed jet discharges against the pressure the flow passes zero at, also when the hole is fed from a flowing `MomentumChamberNode`. Both forms vanish together, so the flow passes zero continuously instead of jumping by most of a jet across the 3 Pa blend, and `is_ingesting` agrees with the sign of the solved flow. `dP_drive`, the throat Mach and tap blocks, and the second-law control volume read the same pair of pressures.
  - **What moves.** Probes inside the band flip from ingesting to blowing (50 of 50). Mixed-supply chamber probes converge more often (31 -> 43 of 43). Outside the band the solved flows move only within root tolerance (<= 1.6e-8 relative). A hole into a plenum, and a plain `OrificeElement`, are unchanged.
