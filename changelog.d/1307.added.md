- `fanno_rayleigh_choked_alternative` says whether choked flow also holds where a cooled Fanno-Rayleigh duct runs subsonic, for given boundary values (#1307).
  - Cooling raises a duct's Pt, so its needed drive can rise from zero flow to a peak and fall back. Below that peak the duct is bistable.
  - The jump to the choke as Tt_exit or L moves is a fold of the model, so no root choice can remove it. On a 1 cm duct from 400 K it is a 7.9x step in G at 1.5 Pa.
  - `fanno_rayleigh_channel_flow` keeps the start-up root, the state a slow ramp from rest reaches, now documented as the convention.
  - The verdict holds the exit Tt and the end pressures fixed, so it is a kernel answer, not a network one. Reporting a network's other solutions is #1338.
