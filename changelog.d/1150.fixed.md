- **A stationed `CrossflowSegmentElement` fed straight from a node at rest now pays the acceleration head (#1150).** With no `entry_K`, such a feed is a lossless entry, the same as `entry_K=0`. A node at rest is a plenum, a combustor or a boundary.
  - **Before:** the incompressible row had no term for that feed. The gates also disagreed on what a station is: the reservoir ends and the duct ends counted only the segment's own `from_kappa` or `entry_K`, while the exit head into a boundary also counted a neighbour's station. Two boundaries 2 kPa apart, two 0.05 m segments of 2e-4 m^2:
    - A segment with its own to-station and no `entry_K` passed 3.8798e-2 kg/s forward against -1.2840e-2 reversed, incompressible. Compressible it passed 1.2824e-2 both ways, so the regime gap was +203 %.
    - A segment whose only station is a neighbour's passed 3.8798e-2 incompressible and 3.7111e-2 compressible on its boundary-fed leg, and 1.284e-2 on the other leg (2.9-3.0x). These are B1 reversed (it leaves the station the previous segment's `to_kappa` registers) and B2 forward (it arrives at the next segment's from-station). Into a `MomentumChamberNode` it passed 9.20e-3 forward against -3.24e-2 reversed.
  - **Now:** every gate reads one "has a station" predicate, which counts a neighbour's station. At a node at rest, the incompressible row carries the entry head. A segment's end at a neighbour's station takes the node's `P` as the duct static in both regimes, the same as an end at its own station. Each layout above passes 1.28403e-2 incompressible and 1.28241e-2 compressible in both directions, a regime gap of +0.126 % (+0.191 % into the chamber).
    - A segment with no `entry_K` gives the same residuals and Jacobian as `entry_K=0`, bit for bit.
    - A segment whose only station is a neighbour's flows within 1.4e-9 of one whose station is its own.
  - **Compressible:** a segment whose only station is a neighbour's moves in both regimes, because its duct ends now close at that station. B1 forward moved by +2.1e-5 relative and B1 reversed by 2.9x. A segment with its own station and no `entry_K` is unchanged in the compressible regime.
  - **Unchanged:** segments that set `entry_K`, and bare segments (#1138), give bit-identical residuals and Jacobians. A segment fed from a moving `MomentumChamberNode` gets no term. It is outside the rule and keeps its incompressible gap.
  - **Who is affected:** only hand-built networks. No shipped builder or stress family creates such a segment: `EffusionLiner`, the impingement gap, `jet_chain` and `bleed_station` all set `entry_K` or a station at each end.
