- **One C++ call composes `PressureLossElement`'s loss models (`pressure_loss_compose`, #725).** The four built-in correlations (`ConstantFractionLoss`, `LinearThetaFractionLoss`, `ConstantHeadLoss`, `LinearThetaHeadLoss`) resolve to a `cb.PressureLossModel` (`pressure_loss_model()`), and `cb.pressure_loss_compose(model, cb.PressureLossInputs(...))` returns xi with every analytic partial (`cb.PressureLossResult`; `cb.PressureLossKind`, `cb.PRESSURE_LOSS_INPUT_THETA`; `include/combustor_loss.h`).
  - The model's own inputs are a generic vector `x`, theta first, so a future loss map over theta and fuel splits plugs in as another kind.
  - It is an internal composition function. Element options, results, diagnostics and the solver's residuals are unchanged; a user callable keeps its differenced path.
