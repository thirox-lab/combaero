- **A film's undiluted gas takes its node's wall heat at the current point (#1169).** When a wall heats or cools a film's discharge node, the undiluted gas temperature `T_inf` that the film's effectiveness is defined against includes that heat. Heat a walled duct gives up before the holes cools the gas that reaches them.
  - **Before:** the mix read the wall heat of the previous propagation pass, or 0 on a fresh solver, and nothing flagged the read for a repeat pass. Converged values were correct only because another lagged read forced a repeat. The Jacobian left out d(wall heat)/dx in `T_inf`, the film's `T_aw` and the backside rows the film reaches (scaled FD error 1.7e-4 on the issue's network).
  - **Now:** the node's walls are evaluated before the mix, and their heat is relayed into `T_inf` (FD error 6e-8). Converged results match the previous ones to the propagation settle tolerance.
