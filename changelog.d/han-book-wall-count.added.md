Fig. 4.88 is after Chandra and Cook (1994), a square channel with 0-4 ribbed walls. Fig. 4.90 is after Chandra, Niland and Han (1997), a 1:2 rectangle with ribs on the wide or the narrow walls. 41 readings carry a per-value flag where the symbols overlap, and they are scored apart from the clean ones. The book does not print rib height, pitch or Reynolds number for these rigs, so those values are recorded as unknown.
