- **A bleed station's straight-run momentum follows the stream that arrives (#1178).** `station_half_drop` gives the side stream an axial velocity of kappa times the arriving flow's (kappa = 0.75 is Bassett's bleed). It used the station's from-side flow m_a for every flow sign. In reversed flow that is the stream that LEAVES, which added kappa (m_a - m_b)^2 / (rho A^2) of static rise along the flow: 393 Pa and 180 Pa at two stations of the #1157 network. The term is now kappa (m_a - m_b) (max(m_a, 0) - max(-m_b, 0)), the arriving streams' mass-weighted mean velocity times rho A.
  - **Forward flow is unchanged, bit for bit**: value and partials, and the residuals and Jacobian of the forward networks checked.
  - **Reversed flow** takes m_b, so a bleed chain built backwards is now its own mirror to round-off (it was off by 5e-4 relative in segment flow, 4.5 Pa in station pressure).
  - **A station the flows meet at** (m_a > 0 > m_b) takes kappa (m_a^2 - m_b^2). That is an extrapolation, because Bassett's data are for dividing flow only. Two equal reservoirs feeding a bleed station now split its hole flow equally; before, one side's flow reversed.
  - **A station the flow leaves both ways** gets no term.
  - **The term is continuous across each sign change but not smooth** (C0). Smoothing it (widths 1e-4 to 1e-3 kg/s) changed no outcome in the networks tried, so it ships C0.
