- **A `MomentumChamberNode` as a crossflow station is refused at validate instead of crashing the solve.** An `ImpingementPlateElement`'s node, a `CrossflowSegmentElement` (or `ImpingementCrossflowElement`) from-station, or a to-station with a next segment, wired to a `MomentumChamberNode` failed the first evaluation with `ValueError: too many values to unpack`. It now raises a `ValueError` naming the element and the node: station nodes must be `PlenumNode`s holding the channel's static pressure, and a stream is modelled either lumped (a `MomentumChamberNode`) or distributed (the crossflow chain), not both. A chain's exit segment into a `MomentumChamberNode` (no to-station) is a terminal node, not a station, and still solves. `PlenumNode` stations are bit-identical to before.
