- **A legacy `TeeJunctionElement` with a reversed leg feeds each node the streams that arrive at it (#1231).** The tee is now fed like a reversed `MultiPortChamberBase` junction (#517).
  - **Before:** the tee handed its sink nodes signed leg flows. A merging tee whose branch leg drew flow out of the common node mixed that leg into the node as a negative stream. The branch node, which the leg flows into, received nothing and kept the 300 K fallback. In a random sweep of 200 merging tees, 18 converged roots had the common node outside its inputs' temperature range, up to 17,800 K from 300-900 K. The energy ledger closed only because the signed mean subtracted the fallback state again.
  - **Now:** each port node mixes only the legs arriving at it, and the node a reversed leg flows into receives that leg's stream, with analytic partials. The heat-fade and T <= 0 checks read the same inflows. The residuals of a tee with every leg in its declared direction are unchanged bit for bit, and so is every network without a legacy tee.
- **The legacy `TeeJunctionElement` has a second-law check (#545, #1231).** It used to be `not_checked`. Its control volume takes each leg arriving at the tee at its port node's state and each leg it delivers at the mix it delivers. In the sweep it fails 38 roots that create flow work, among them a branch-reversed root that delivered air at 203.5 kPa from a 200 kPa supply.
  - The #517 junctions' control volume books a delivered leg the same way. Their port chambers take no other stream, so their verdicts are unchanged.
- **The legacy tee's Jacobian follows its port nodes' gas constant and gamma (#1235).** The kernel takes each port node's R and gamma as numbers, and their dependence on the node's composition (and gamma's on its T0) was missing from the rows. A side stream of another gas into the common chamber of a forward branching tee put the side stream's column 2 % off; a branching tee with its straight leg reversed was 0.2-0.7 % off on its flow columns. Both now match a central difference to 1e-8. New `cb.specific_gas_constant_dY(X)` and `cb.isentropic_expansion_coefficient_dT_dY(T, X)` carry the gradients from C++, and `CompressibleTeeResult` reports `dR{0,1}_dRgas_*` and `dR{0,1}_dgamma_*`.
