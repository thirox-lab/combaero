- **A bare `CrossflowSegmentElement` now loses its exit head the way a `ChannelElement` does (#1138).** A bare segment is one with no station at either end and no `entry_K`.
  - **Before:** such a segment never paid its exit dynamic head into a `PressureBoundary`, in either flow direction or regime. The opt-out of #588 covered every segment, although its reason (station nodes discharge at static continuity) only holds for a segment with a station or an entry. At 2 kPa the bare segment passed 3.72e-2 kg/s into a boundary, 2.9x the 1.28e-2 kg/s of a `ChannelElement` between the same nodes, with a regime gap of +5.6 %. Boundary to boundary it passed 4.83e-2 against 1.32e-2 kg/s, with a gap of +8.0 %.
  - **Now:** its exit follows the `ChannelElement`'s. The head is lost into a `PressureBoundary` whether the segment discharges forward into its to-node or reversed into its from-node. The flow matches the channel's (1.281e-2 kg/s into a boundary, 1.323e-2 boundary to boundary) and the regime gap drops to +0.18 % / +0.16 %. Into a `PlenumNode` or a `MomentumChamberNode`, where a channel recovers the head, the bare segment is unchanged.
  - Segments with a station or an `entry_K` are unchanged, and so is a segment leaving a station that its neighbour registers (the previous segment's `to_kappa`): it is fed at the station's static pressure with its gas already moving, so charging the head there would count it twice. Their residuals and Jacobians are bit-identical. No shipped builder (impingement gap, `EffusionLiner`) creates a bare segment, so only hand-built ones move.
