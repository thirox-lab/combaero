- **The equilibrium Jacobian of a lean burn without steam or CO2 is no longer refused (#1293).** `combustion_equilibrium_and_jacobian` raised "no converged trace state" where a lean burn lacks one of steam or CO2 and only that carrier is fed. The value path was fine.
  - **Where it was refused:**
    - pure CO fuel (or CO with CO2) in dry air: 74 of 348 dry, lean CO states in a 10,000-state draw;
    - H2 or NH3 burning lean in an oxidizer with no CO2, such as O2/N2.
  - **Cause:** take pure CO fuel in dry air. The burn holds CO2 and none of CO, H2 or steam, and only steam is fed, by any hydrogen-bearing inlet species. With steam and CO2 alone the shift can run neither forward (no CO) nor back (no H2). So every trace solve of the one-sided Jacobian converged with it still left out, and the ladder took that as a failure.
  - **Now:** where the shift is the only reaction (no hydrocarbon) and cannot run either way with the fed species seeded, its extent is boxed at zero an instant later. The derivatives with it held there are exact, and they are kept. Each column matches a finite difference to 1e-5 of itself, and dT/dP and dT/dT_in match theirs.
    - **Draws:** 300 drawn dry, lean CO states (all now have a Jacobian), and an independent review's 73 gained CO states and 27 H2/NH3 states.
    - **Still refused:** with a hydrocarbon present, reactions can combine where none runs alone.
  - **Unchanged:** every other state's value and Jacobian. A 6000-state draw over eleven fuels and blends in air gains 47 such Jacobians and moves nothing else. So does an independent 8000-state draw: 73 gained, 7927 bit-identical.
