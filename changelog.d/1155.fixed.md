- **A compressible crossflow segment's `dP_momentum` reports what its row applies (#1155).** `CrossflowSegmentElement` (and `ImpingementCrossflowElement`, the `EffusionLiner` and `ImpingementArray` segments built on it).
  - **Before:** the compressible report summed the reservoir entry's full (1 + K) q. The mass-flow row never adds that: the dynamic head is in its outlet closure, and only the loss K/(1 + K) of the entry goes on the outlet Pt. A segment with `entry_K=0` reported 1759 Pa where the same row with no `entry_K` reported 4e-14 Pa (1664 Pa against -3e-13 Pa behind a plenum). Their residuals were already identical (#1150, #1188).
  - **Now:** the report sums each term's share in the row. The pressure-form row (incompressible, or a ribbed or pin-fin surface) is unchanged and reports every term in full. The compressible row reports the station halves and the entry loss K q, so no `entry_K` and `entry_K=0` report the same thing. Residuals and Jacobians are bit-identical over every stress family.
  - **Changed values:** compressible segments that set `entry_K` drop the entry head q from `dP_momentum`. These are the `EffusionLiner` inlet segment (`entry_K=0.5` by default) and an `ImpingementArray` bypass inlet (`bypass_entry_K=0` by default). Incompressible reports and the shipped GUI examples are unchanged.
