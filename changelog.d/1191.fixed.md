- A warm-started cooled channel no longer keeps a later fall of its exit-pressure excess (#1191). On a cooled duct whose needed drive rises again after a fold's valley, `fanno_rayleigh_channel_flow` with a warm start (`G_guess > 0`, a channel element's previous flux) could return a later crossing than the cold call's first fall, so the solved flux depended on the solver's history (issue duct: cold 0.666323 Gc, warm from 0.95-0.999 Gc 0.957556 Gc). A cooled warm root is now checked by the cold scan run up to the warm bracket's lower end, and a fall found below it is returned instead. Heated and adiabatic ducts and cold calls are unchanged; stress-family F and J are bit-identical. Cost: about 22 more marches per cooled warm call (+4 % serial stress, +31 % Marek-Juhasz runner, +42 % C3X vane example). One limit remains, a fold hidden between one scan's bracket ends but not the other's (#1206): a warm and a cold call can still differ, either way, on 0.4-0.8 % of checked calls in random cooled-duct sweeps over all friction models; where the check finds a fall the cold scan misses, the warm call is the right one.
