- **Ribbed-channel pressure drop: Han's Fanning factor, weighted by the ribbed walls (#1106).** A ribbed `ChannelElement` (`ConvectiveSurface(model=RibbedModel(...))`) applied the rib set's four-sided Fanning `f` as a Darcy factor and ignored `n_ribbed_walls`.
  - **Before:** `dP = f (L/D) rho V^2/2` with Han's four-sided `f` for 1, 2 or 4 ribbed walls -- about 2.2x low for two ribbed walls (2884 Pa against Han's 6284 Pa at Re 5.7e4), and 59% low across Han's own two-wall friction data.
  - **Now:** the drop uses the channel's Darcy factor `4 [phi f + (1 - phi) 0.046 Re^-0.2]`, Han's own area weighting (Han and Park 1988 Eq. 6) with `phi` the ribbed wall fraction. For two walls this is Han's measured passage `fbar`; the element scores -0.9% bias, 5.9% MAE on Han's data (fidelity, 119 points). Ribbed drops roughly double, so ribbed networks pass less flow at a given pressure ratio.
  - New C++/Python `rib_channel_friction(set, geometry, Re, n_ribbed_walls)` returns `(f_D, df_D/dRe)`, and `rib_ribbed_wall_fraction(W_H, n_ribbed_walls)` returns the ribbed wall fraction. The residual row now carries the Re (mass-flow and viscosity) derivative of the smooth walls' friction.
  - One and four ribbed walls are outside the two-opposite-wall configuration every rib set was measured on, so they now set `extrapolated` (`surface_extrapolated`).
  - `RibbedModel` checks `n_ribbed_walls` when it is built: an integral float (`2.0`) is taken as that count, and anything outside 1, 2 or 4 (including `3`, `2.5` or a bool) raises `ValueError` there instead of at solve time.
