- **The per-element hole-Cd keywords are deprecated and will be removed in 0.13.0; `hole_cd=HoleCd(...)` replaces them.** `correlation=` and `Cd=` on `EffusionPlateElement`, `FilmCoolingElement`, `SlotFilmElement`, `ImpingementPlateElement` and `ImpingementArray` (`HoleCd(correlation=..., Cd=...)`), and `cd_crossflow=` on the impingement row and array (`HoleCd(exit_crossflow=...)`).
  - Each still builds the same element, results and Jacobian bit for bit, with one `DeprecationWarning` per construction (one per `ImpingementArray`, not per row) naming the keywords and the `HoleCd` that replaces them. Passing `hole_cd=` together with any of them raises `ValueError`.
  - Not deprecated: `OrificeElement`'s own `correlation` and `Cd` (its metering keywords; a `HoleCd` field left at None takes them, and one that disagrees raises), and the read-only attributes `cd_inclination`, `cd_exit_crossflow` and `cd_crossflow`, which report the models in use. The GUI's saved-file fields are unchanged.
