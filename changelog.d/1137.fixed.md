- **A crossflow segment's reservoir entry (`entry_K`) charges its drop at the density of the gas it carries, the reservoir's (#1137).** The entry drop `(1 + K_in) m^2 / (2 rho A^2)` took the TO-node's gas: its T0 and composition. Where that node also takes another stream it is a mixed or burnt gas, so the entry loss was charged at the wrong density, in both regimes (the incompressible row and the compressible duct ends' `K_in/(1 + K_in)` share). It now takes the reservoir's T0 and composition at the duct's static pressure at the to-end, as Idelchik's entrance coefficients are defined (on the dynamic head of the duct's own stream). The incompressible exit head into a `MomentumChamberNode` moves with it, so the two heads still cancel to the entry loss `K_in q`.
  - **Regime gap** (incompressible - compressible flow), one entry segment: into a CH4-burning `CombustorNode`, -29.5 / -20.6 % -> +0.09 / +0.19 % at 2 / 8 kPa (entry_K 0.5) and -37.1 / -24.5 % -> +0.15 / +0.40 % (entry_K 0); into a plenum that also takes a 900 K stream, -5.1 % -> +0.002 to +0.011 %. Into a merge chamber the gap no longer drifts with the side stream's temperature (it crossed zero near 900 K); what remains is O(Ma^2), from the merge face, and it still depends on the mixed gas (+0.15 % at 8 kPa with a 900 K side stream, +0.06 % with 1500 K, +0.18 % with the supply's 300 K).
  - **An `ImpingementArray` bypass at a temperature other than the jets'** enters a station plenum where the jets join, so that station's gas is mixed too. The bypass flow moves by -0.78 / -1.58 % incompressible at a 420 / 700 K bypass (300 K jets), and by -0.35 / -0.72 % compressible at `bypass_entry_K` 0.5. Its regime gap is now the cold bypass's (-1.27 % at that drive, falling as Ma^2); before it drifted with the bypass temperature, to +0.25 % at 700 K.
  - **Compressible flows move** where the to-node's gas differs from the reservoir's and entry_K > 0: +24 % / +13 % into the burning combustor (entry_K 0.5, 2 / 8 kPa), +4.4 % into a hot merge chamber, +2.6 % into a hot merge plenum.
  - **Unchanged:** entries whose to-node holds the reservoir's gas (a station plenum without other streams or with streams at the reservoir's state, a single-stream chamber or plenum, a non-reacting combustor), to round-off in the to-node's mixed composition; and every reversed flow, where the entry term is zero.
  - **Still open (#1146):** the entry term, like the chamber exit head, has no partial in the reservoir's composition. A reservoir of fixed composition (a boundary, air plenums) is exact; a reservoir whose composition moves with the flows (a burning combustor feeding the segment) leaves an FD error of 8e-4 incompressible and 3e-4 compressible on the segment row.
