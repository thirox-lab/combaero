- **The equilibrium combustor's slope at a zero-flow stream is the one-sided limit along that stream (#1241).** Where a stream at exactly zero flow feeds a species the burned state lacks (fuel arriving at a fuel-free mix, air at a fuel-only one, carbon at a carbon-free burn), the combustor's flow column is now the slope along that stream's own composition.
  - **Why a matrix could not hold it:** in dry air the slopes along two fuels do not add (CH4 with CO at 335 K: 11 %).
  - **Before:** the column was off by up to 4x, and of the wrong sign for CO arriving at rich NH3 (-18.7 against 816 K per unit mole fraction).
  - **Now:** it matches a forward difference to 2e-5 wherever the difference resolves the limit.
  - **The trace ladder** now steps down until its derivatives settle (`REFORMING_JACOBIAN_LIMIT_RTOL`, 1e-8), so J at zero flow equals the slope an instant later, at 1e-40 kg/s of air, to 2.5e-9.
  - **Where a difference cannot check it:** that limit can lie far below any step a solver takes. Air reaching a fuel-only blend at 300 K: N2 goes completely to NH3, and the first oxygen's products reform, only below about 1e-26 kg/s of air. A difference over 1e-15 to 1e-11 kg/s sees a slope 90 times the limit's.
- **A fuel trace in warm air converges (#1241).** With reforming always on, a 1e-20 to 1e-13 trace of fuel in air at 500 to 900 K was refused (151 states). The heavy alkanes' equilibrium amounts there are no double (n-heptane about 1e-391), and Newton's log step underflowed. A key whose equilibrium amount is below the normal double range is now held at exactly 0, and its reaction runs to completion.
- **The NH3 start no longer throws below the representable range (#1241).** At the 10 K temperature floor the adiabatic search probes, very rich NH3 without N2 has a root extent of about e^-1000. The scalar NH3 start raised after 200 iterations, which aborted the whole equilibrium. It now hands its bracketed iterate to the joint solve, which decides convergence.
