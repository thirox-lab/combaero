- **An unreachable fuel-controller target is refused before the solve where the inputs prove it (#1114).** A `FuelBoundary` target outside its reachable window used to be refused only after a full Newton search and its retries: 0.5-5.9 s (1.8 s mean) on the nine such draws of the `fuel_controller` stress family, seeds 0-99.
  - **Now:** when the combustor's inflows are fixed by the inputs -- every oxidizer source a fixed-flow `MassFlowBoundary`, reaching the combustor through plain orifices, lossless links and plenums with no wall or energy boundary, and the combustor with one outflow -- the combustor's Tt depends on the fuel/air ratio alone, and a target proven outside the window is refused in milliseconds with the same reason, prefixed "Refused before the solve". Proven: above the unsmoothed stoichiometric peak (lean, or rich with complete combustion), below the Tt with no injected fuel (lean), and a rich target below the oxidizer's Tt on either method, refused by rule (see the fuel-as-coolant entry).
  - **Unchanged:** air through a pressure boundary, and a rich target above the peak on an equilibrium combustor; these still go through the solve. Converged solutions are bit-identical.
