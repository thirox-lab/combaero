- **Equilibrium combustion now converges for rich, fuel-only and very lean burns (#1262).** The reforming + WGS solve stalled in three regimes, and its result was used anyway: where the oxygen is nearly gone, where a hydrocarbon that equilibrium holds was never made, and in very lean, cold burns with trace fuel left over. That held for 201 of 375 methane trace-oxygen and very rich states (300-900 K), for 1343 of 3240 states in a mixed random draw, and for 178 of 882 lean ones.
  - **How:** the solve now picks, from the same reactions, a basis whose keys are the scarcest species. A reaction driven toward an absent species is run by a trace, which seeds that species in its own stoichiometry, and the solve is repeated; one still driven that way after that, or any element imbalance, means not converged. Where an inner solve at a bracket end fails, the adiabatic temperature is polished on converged solves instead of being refused.
  - **What converges:** every state of those methane and lean sets, every state of a 4000-state draw from phi 1e-5 to 3000 over six fuels, and every state of a grid of seven fuels at phi 3 to 3000, 250-800 K and 1-20 bar. In pyrolysis, where H2 is scarcer than the second hydrocarbon (cold heptane at phi 1000), H2 is exchanged with the two most abundant hydrocarbons; before that, half the states near cold heptane at phi 1000 and 10 bar were refused, and T scattered by up to 4.6 K with a 1e-11 change in the inlet temperature. Still not converging: very lean burns below about 245 K (phi up to about 7e-4, #1285), and heptane beyond phi of about 2e6.
  - **Agreement with references:** within 0.05 K of an independent Gibbs minimum on combaero's thermo. Against Cantera on the same C1-C3 species, 1e-7 relative in composition and 4e-5 K, where combaero's C4+ species stay below 1e-8.
  - **States that move:**
    - States that did not converge before move by a median of 29 K, up to 318 K. Rich burns at phi 3 to 30 move most, a median of 145 K.
    - Converged states move by at most 1e-11 K, except fuel with a trace of oxygen: it now holds the ethane and hydrogen equilibrium implies, up to 8.3 K at 900 K.
  - **No Jacobian at a non-root:** `combustion_equilibrium_and_jacobian`, and through it the equilibrium combustor and `combustion_from_streams(method="equilibrium")`, raise where the equilibrium does not converge. The network treats that as an unphysical probe.
