- **New validation dataset: total cooling effectiveness of effusion liners in a reacting flow at 5 and 10 bar.** `validation/cooling/data/behrendt2008/` holds Behrendt, Lengyel, Hassa and Gerendas (2008), GT2008-51191. It has 9 laterally averaged `eta_tot(x/d)` curves for 20 and 30 deg effusion plates behind a kerosene combustor, with coolant temperature, pressure drop and hot-gas pressure each varied in turn. Hot-side heat-flux points are filed but flagged as biased by the sensor. The curves were extracted from the PDF vector paths. Three curves printed twice agree to 1e-5. They are cross-source accuracy data for `EffusionPlateElement`'s overall effectiveness. They are not a gas-side closure, because there is no adiabatic wall temperature. The extraction record is `validation/cooling/extractions/behrendt_2008_effusion_eta_tot.md`.
