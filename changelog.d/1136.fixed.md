- **A crossflow chain's end at a `CombustorNode` is an end at rest in both regimes (#1136).** A combustor holds Pt = P (`state_at_rest`), like a plenum, but the chain decided "at rest" by asking for a `PlenumNode` or a boundary. Compressible, a segment discharging into a combustor matched its exit total to the combustor's Pt and recovered its dynamic head; incompressible, it kept static continuity forward and, reversed, left out the acceleration out of the combustor that a plenum exit carries. On a one-station bleed probe the regimes differed by -21 % forward and +179 % reversed at 2 kPa (a single-segment chain: -31.6 % / -47.5 %).
  - **Now:** every reader of a chain's terminal end asks the node's own `state_at_rest` (the compressible terminal, the incompressible reversed exit and exit head, the start guess). A non-reacting combustor end solves a plenum end's flow, at the exit (bleed probe regime gap +0.06 % forward, +0.17 % reversed at 2 kPa, with the reversed exit density fixed in `1136-reversed-exit`) and at a reservoir-entry from-node the reversed flow runs into (compressible reversed -6.61e-2 -> -3.49e-2 kg/s, the plenum's). The compressible forward flow into a combustor falls by 21 % on the bleed probe (the head is lost by static continuity, the chain's convention at every node at rest).
  - **Not yet:** a burning or mixing combustor exit is still regime-inconsistent (-21 to -29 % on one entry segment into a CH4 flame): the forward entry term takes the to-node's burnt-gas density (#1137).
  - **Unchanged:** plenum, boundary and `MomentumChamberNode` ends, bit for bit in x, F and J, apart from the reversed exit density fix. A `CombustorNode` station is still refused.
