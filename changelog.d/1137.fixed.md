- **A crossflow segment's reservoir entry (`entry_K`) charges its drop at the density of the gas it carries, the reservoir's (#1137).** The entry drop `(1 + K_in) m^2 / (2 rho A^2)` took the TO-node's gas: its T0 and composition. Where that node also takes another stream it is a mixed or burnt gas, so the entry loss was charged at the wrong density, in both regimes (the incompressible row and the compressible duct ends' `K_in/(1 + K_in)` share). It now takes the reservoir's T0 and composition at the duct's static pressure at the to-end, as Idelchik's entrance coefficients are defined (on the dynamic head of the duct's own stream). The incompressible exit head into a `MomentumChamberNode` moves with it, so the two heads still cancel to the entry loss `K_in q`.
  - **Regime gap** (incompressible - compressible flow), one entry segment: into a CH4-burning `CombustorNode`, -29.5 / -20.6 % -> +0.09 / +0.19 % at 2 / 8 kPa (entry_K 0.5) and -37.1 / -24.5 % -> +0.15 / +0.40 % (entry_K 0); into a plenum that also takes a 900 K stream, -5.1 % -> +0.002 to +0.011 %. Into a merge chamber the gap is now the one-gas chain's O(Ma^2) gap (+0.06 / +0.15 % at 2 / 8 kPa); before it drifted with the side stream's temperature.
  - **Compressible flows move** where the to-node's gas differs from the reservoir's and entry_K > 0: +24 % / +13 % into the burning combustor (entry_K 0.5, 2 / 8 kPa), +4.4 % into a hot merge chamber, +2.6 % into a hot merge plenum.
  - **Unchanged:** entries whose to-node holds the reservoir's gas (a station plenum, a single-stream chamber or plenum, a non-reacting combustor), and every reversed flow, where the entry term is zero.
