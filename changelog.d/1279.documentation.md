- **`dh_dT`, `ds_dT` and `dcp_dT` are documented as molar (#1279).** Their docstrings said J/(kg K) and J/(kg K^2); they return the molar values, as `units_data.h` states: dh/dT is the molar cp, ds/dT is cp/T. A test now checks every public function's docstring against `units_data.h` for the J/kg versus J/mol basis; these three were the only mismatches.
