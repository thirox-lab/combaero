- **An impingement row's Jacobian inside the crossflow Cd's cap band (#1248).** Rogers & Hersh's jet-crossflow law joins the hole's own Cd0 smoothly over +/-10 % of it, and inside that band its Cd reads Cd0, and through it the hole's Reynolds number and so the row's own flow. The composed Cd (C++ `hole_cd_compose`) dropped the hole's partials wherever the law acted, so a row in the band (an `IdelchikThick` or other Re-dependent hole, a bypass crossflow) had every Jacobian column about 0.8 % off: the self-consistent flow divides by 1 - K dCd/dm, and dCd/dm was missing.
  - **Now:** `rogers_hersh_1976_cd` returns `dCd_dCd0` (1 where capped, 0 on the fit, f - u df/du in the band) and the composed Cd carries the hole's partials scaled by it. The residual and its roots are unchanged; only the Jacobian moves, and only inside the band.
