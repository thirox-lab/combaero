- `IdealMomentumTeeElement` rows no longer carry round-off noise when no port supplies the junction (every port flowing out, as a Newton iterate can). The kernel formed each receiver's role weight as `m s - m`, a cancellation that kept only 1e-16 / (eps/m)^2 of its digits, and the mean supplied velocity is a ratio of such weights there: up to 7e-5 Pa of noise on 1.8e5 Pa rows, which a finite-difference check read as a Jacobian miss. The weights are now formed without the cancellation; the rows move by round-off only (at most 1.6e-11 Pa at the measured point) and the Jacobian is unchanged (#1250).
