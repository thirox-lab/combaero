- `NetworkRunner.sweep` now solves a row cold when the warm start from the previous row does not converge. The warm seed carries absolute pressures, so a row that shifts the pressure level by more than a fraction of the network's pressure drop could stall from it. Before this fix, every row after a stall inherited the same stale seed, while each of those rows converged cold in about 0.1 s. A sweep row now always gets at least what `solve()` gives for that case. A row's `warm_start` names the case it actually started from (#1054).
