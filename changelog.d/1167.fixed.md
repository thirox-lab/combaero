- **A cooled channel whose needed drive peaks and turns back down is no longer reported choked by a cold call (#1167).** `fanno_rayleigh_channel_flow` without a warm start (`G_guess <= 0`) scans the flux up in ln-2 brackets and takes the first whose exit-pressure excess falls through zero. On a cooled duct the excess can fall through zero and rise above it again inside one bracket. Both ends then read positive, the scan saw no fall, and the duct was reported choked at Gc while a warm start found its first crossing unchoked. Examples (dry air, haaland, static exit): a 3 MPa, 1400 -> 1000 K, 0.03 m duct at drives of 259963-261953 Pa (first crossing 0.975-0.99 Gc), its 1050-1150 K twins in bands of 265-917 Pa, and a 2 bar, 1800 -> 400 K, 0.3 m duct at 98-108 Pa (0.12 Gc). The same 2 bar duct has a second, laminar fold: on its total-pressure exit cold calls read it choked from 13.02 to 14.0 Pa (the fold's peak).
  - **Now:** on a cooled duct (Tt_exit < Tt0) the scan reads the excess's slope at each bracket end, and a bracket with both ends positive whose cubic Hermite interpolant has an interior minimum is searched by Brent's minimiser; at or below zero, the first fall is bracketed there. The last bracket, which ends at the march's sonic flux, is sampled at fluxes 4^-j of that end below it, where those folds sit. A cooled duct's warm bracket widens to at most 6.4 % of its guess, so it cannot straddle a fall, a rise and a later fall; the cold scan decides those. Cold and warm calls agree, with guesses from 0.05 to 1.5 G, through each of these folds: to 1e-11 on the 3 MPa ducts and to ~1e-9 at a few Pa of drive on 2 bar, where the root carries ~2e-10 of rounding either way (the same before). Up to each fold's peak G rises continuously with the drive; past it the duct is choked (or takes the next branch). The derivatives at the new roots match central differences to 1e-4, and a wall-cooled network on one matches an all-row finite-difference Jacobian to 2.6e-5 in scaled variables.
  - **Unchanged:** every heated or adiabatic duct, and every cooled cold call with no fold: F and J are bit-identical on 948 fresh evaluations of the stress families with Fanno-Rayleigh ducts. No call in the Python suite, the stress families (seeds 0:40), the examples or the cooling validation runners met a fold, so no converged result moves.
  - **Cost:** only on cooled ducts. A cold call adds one march per bracket (about 11) for the slopes, the last bracket's 15 samples when it is reached with both ends positive (a choked duct), and a minimisation where the interpolant turns; the minimiser converges fully on a minimum that stays positive. Measured against main: +29 % on an unchoked cold call, +82 % on a choked one (which goes cold on every call), 3.7-4.4x on a choked duct with a fold. Warm unchoked calls and heated ducts are unchanged. A serial stress A/B over the six families with these ducts: identical outcomes on all 240 draws, +1.9 % wall time (+9 % on `hotcold`), final norms differ on 46 draws, from warm calls that now go cold (kernel flux 4e-14 apart); on six of them checked, the converged unknowns move by at most 8e-12 relative.
  - **Still possible:**
    - Two turns between bracket ends whose values and slopes show none: within one ln-2 bracket (half of one where its ends straddle zero, #1190), or near the choke within one gap of the 4^-j grid (up to a factor of 8 of the distance to the end for the first gap).
    - A warm start within 6.4 % above a later fall returns that fall. One fold is enough where the needed drive rises again after its valley, as it often does toward the choke: on a 1.286 MPa, 1984.9 -> 1248.2 K, 0.159 m, 5.87 mm duct with a total-pressure exit, at 6797.7 Pa, the cold call gives the first fall, 0.6663 Gc, and warm calls from 0.95-0.999 Gc give 0.9576 Gc.
