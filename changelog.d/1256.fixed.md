- **A mass-flow row keeps a finite slope at zero drive (#1256, #1209, #1193).** A compressible orifice row (and every hole, film, effusion, impingement and effective-area connection, which share its kernel) had exactly zero partials in its pressures at a zero pressure drop, and between about 1.7e-10 and 2e-9 Pa its flow was a round-off staircase while J read about -22. A compressible duct (`ChannelElement`, `CrossflowSegmentElement`) at exactly zero Fanno drive had a zero pressure slope too. The Brayton cycle's start puts two orifices at exactly zero drop, so its cold solve converged or not on a 1e-10 Pa change of one start pressure.
  - **Now:** below `kJetDriveFloor` = 1e-3 Pa of drive (`include/flow_drive_floor.h`) the nozzle and Jobson orifice kernels take the flow at the floor times the C1 odd cubic the Fanno kernel already used below its 1 Pa floor, so the slope at zero is finite and the staircase is gone; from the floor up the flow is bit-identical. The Fanno and Fanno-Rayleigh kernels return their cubic's slope at exactly zero drive, and `ChannelElement` takes the mean of the forward and reverse one-sided slopes there. The Brayton start converges on 40/40 seeded perturbations and 14/14 sub-nanopascal offsets (35/40 and 10/14 before).
