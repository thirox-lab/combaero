- **A mixer mixes the streams that arrive (#1232).** `mixer_from_streams_and_jacobians` and the combustor kernels weight the streams with `m_i > 0`, or every stream when none arrives (the reverse mean of #1224). A stream leaving beside an arriving one moves nothing.
  - **Before:** `Y`, `h` and `Pt` were the signed mean `sum m_i q_i / sum m_i` over all streams. With streams of both signs it left the range of its inputs and had a pole at zero net flow. +0.1 kg/s at 800 K against -0.12 kg/s at 400 K gave `Tt_mix = -1700 K`, and a net flow of 1e-9 kg/s gave 1.2e10 K. A negative mean `Y` was clamped, so its partials no longer matched a difference, and `calc_T_from_h` raised in 17 of 20,000 random mixed-sign mixers. In the band `|m| <= 1e-12` stream 0's state stood in. For `0 < m <= 1e-12` that meant the averaged `h` with stream 0's `Y`: with CH4 as stream 0, `Tt_mix` was 1360 K against 664 K just outside the band.
  - **Now:** the mean is over streams of one sign, so `Y` stays in the simplex and `h` and `Pt` stay within the inputs at any nonzero flow. There is no pole, no clamp and no near-zero band. `Y`, `h` and `Pt` and every partial come from that one mean down to zero flow. Only a mixer with no flow at all (every `m_i = 0`) still falls back to stream 0's state with zero partials. When the last arriving stream crosses zero, the state jumps from that stream's own state to the leaving streams' mean.
  - No network reaches a mixer with streams of both signs since #1231. In the stress sweep (16 families, seeds 0:40), the legacy-tee sweep (200 cases), the junction network runner (3 models, 7,638 records) and the test suite, every such call comes from tests that call the kernel directly. The stress and tee outcomes, the converged roots and the junction records and RMSE are unchanged. F and J are unchanged bit for bit at 1,173 stress-network points. Only points with a node's total flow in `0 < m <= 1e-12` change, and none of those is a converged root.
- **`calc_T_from_h` (and `_s`, `_u`) converge above 1.1e9 K (#1232).** The Newton stop test compared the step with `tol` (1e-6 K) where T's own resolution is coarser, so a probe at h of order 1e14 J/kg threw "no convergence in 50 iterations" (an AR/CO mixture at 5e11 K). Mixer heat at the heat floor's peak still reaches such h: a 1e8 W probe on a nearly stagnant node. The stop test now takes a few ulps of T where that is coarser than `tol`. Below that T the inverse is unchanged.
