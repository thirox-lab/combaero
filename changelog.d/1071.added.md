- **`EffusionPlateElement(radiation=GasRadiation(beam_length=...))`: opt-in gas radiation on the plate's hot face (#1071).** Until now only a `ThermalWall` face could carry radiation, and the plate owns its wall, so it had none. The plate now takes the same `GasRadiation` spec: Lefebvre & Ballal Eq. 8.7, with the emissivity of Eq. 8.11. C++'s `wall_radiation_sides` solves it together with the wall, so no outer iteration is needed. The gas radiates at `T_gas`, the chamber's static pressure and that stream's composition, unless the spec fixes them. The film lowers only the convective driving temperature. New diagnostics: `q_rad_hot` [W/m^2], which is part of `q_wall`, and `eps_g`. They are 0 without coolant, and NaN on a plenum discharge, where the imposed `gas_heat_flux` stays the whole gas side. A `CasingRadiation` is refused. With radiation off, results are bit-identical to before.
- **Behrendt et al. (2008) is now also scored with gas radiation**, on rows of its own (cross-source accuracy). The inputs are rig boundary conditions: a beam length from the duct section and a 0.9 wall emissivity for the high-emissivity coating. The mean over-prediction falls from +21.7% to +17.6% on the chamber baseline and from +12.6% to +8.9% on the flat plate. The 5-to-10-bar pressure trend improves from 0.68x to 0.84x of the measured one. The coolant-temperature trend is still missed.
