- **A mixer with net reverse flow is the mass-weighted mean, like forward flow (#1224).** `mixer_from_streams_and_jacobians` mixes signed streams.
  - **Before:** at a net reverse total (`m < -1e-12`) it kept `Y_mix` averaged but took `h` and `Pt_mix` from stream 0. Its partials were the mean's, so they disagreed with a difference: for two reverse air streams `dTt/dm` was -4085 K s/kg, and 0 by difference. A non-flowing stream 0 of another gas gave `Tt_mix = -4333 K` (its `h` with the averaged `Y`).
  - **Now:** `Y`, `h` and `Pt` are `sum m_i q_i / m` on either side of zero, and the state and every partial come from that one definition. Reversing every stream gives the same state bit for bit. Forward flow and the near-zero band (`|m| <= 1e-12`, stream 0's state) are unchanged.
  - Only iterates reach it: in the stress sweep, a legacy `TeeJunctionElement`'s combining plenum while a tee leg draws flow out of it. No converged root was net reverse.
