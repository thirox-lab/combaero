- **An incompressible duct's Jacobian at exactly zero flow is the laminar slope (#1156).** This covers a `ChannelElement`, a `CrossflowSegmentElement` and an `ImpingementCrossflowElement` gap, on the smooth path.
  - **Before:** at m = 0 the C++ channel kernel's slope `|m| (f + |m| f' dRe/dm / 2)` evaluated as 0 (f -> 64 / Re, so `|m| f` is 0 * inf). A difference read the laminar slope instead. On a 1 m, 1 cm duct alone between two boundaries, d(row)/dm was 0 against -6.52e4 Pa s/kg, a scaled error of 1.0, and Newton took a singular step from an exact zero start. With `friction_model="petukhov"` the slope was NaN.
  - **Now:** at m = 0 the kernel takes the limit `f_mult f_re_0 mu L / (2 rho A D^2)`, Hagen-Poiseuille's `32 mu L / (rho A D^2)` times the blend's laminar share at Re = 0 (`solver::friction_f_re_at_zero`; 0 for Petukhov, whose f -> 0). It matches the one-sided slopes just off zero to 1e-7. A central difference converges on it linearly in the step, 1.1e-3 to 1.1e-6 relative over h = 1e-6 to 1e-9 kg/s, until the laminar branch's Re 1e-6 floor below h ~ 1e-11 kg/s.
  - **Unchanged:** the residuals everywhere, and the Jacobian at every nonzero flow, are bit-identical. Ribbed and pin-fin channels are also unchanged: their correlations' friction is finite as Re -> 0, so their drop is quadratic there and a slope of 0 is exact.
