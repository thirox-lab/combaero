- **`combustor_residuals_and_jacobians` no longer takes `P` (#1272).** It burns at the streams' mixed total pressure, `mixer_from_streams_and_jacobians(streams).Pt_mix`. That is the one pressure whose stream partials its Jacobian carries (#1244), and the kernel computes it itself. Before, the caller passed `P`, and any value was accepted.
  - The Python binding's arguments after `streams` are keyword-only. A call that still passes `P` positionally raises `TypeError` rather than reading it as `Q`.
  - `CombustorNode` results are unchanged. It now passes no pressure, and its F and J are bit-identical on 13,275 network points.
