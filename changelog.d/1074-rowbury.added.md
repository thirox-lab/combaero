- **`EffusionPlateElement` and `FilmCoolingElement` take `cd_exit_crossflow="rowbury_2001"`, an opt-in exit (external) crossflow term in the discharge coefficient (#1074).** It applies Rowbury, Oldfield & Lock (2001) Eqs. 7-8 on top of the no-crossflow Cd: `1/Cd^2 = 1/Cd0^2 + A k^-B - C`. Here `k` is the perpendicular outlet momentum flux ratio, built from the hole's actual flow and the momentum flux of the discharge `MomentumChamberNode`. `A`, `B` and `C` come from the paper's Table 2. `exit_crossflow_region` picks the set: `"suction_surface"` (the flat-wall default), `"pressure_surface"` or `"leading_edge"`. Shaped film holes take the fan-shaped sets. `exit_crossflow_orientation_deg` is the compound angle. The term works with any correlation, including `"fixed"`, and with or without `cd_inclination`. Crossover is built in: a strongly blowing hole's Cd rises by up to the `-C` floor. Without a flowing chamber the opt-in is refused. The term is held below a `k` floor, where `Cd -> 0` and, for `B > 1`, the hole flow would have no root. The defaults are unchanged bit for bit. Diagnostics, reported only when the term is on: `Cd_no_exit_crossflow`, `Cd_exit_crossflow_ratio`, `exit_crossflow_rho_u2`, `exit_crossflow_k_perp`, `exit_crossflow_delta`, `exit_crossflow_held` and `exit_crossflow_in_range`. The GUI backend reads the three fields from network JSON. There is no Inspector control yet.
- **New C++ `rowbury_2001_exit_crossflow_cd`, `rowbury_2001_coefficients`, `rowbury_2001_k_perp`, `rowbury_2001_k_floor` and `rowbury_2001_in_range` (`crossflow_cd.h`).** All derivatives are analytic.
- **`validation/cooling/exit_crossflow_cd_runner.py` reports fidelity and cross-source accuracy for the term.**
  - **Fidelity, against the authors' own Fig. 9 predictions, read by eye:** within 0.005 at Ma_m 0.6 and within 0.011 at Ma_m 1.2.
  - **Accuracy, cross-source, with the inclination term on:** on Gritsch 1998 the mean error moves from +0.8% to -2.7% at Ma_m 0.3, from +15.9% to +3.6% at Ma_m 0.6, and from +46.8% to +9.9% at Ma_m 1.2. On Hay 1983 at Minf 0.31 it moves from +11.2% to -4.1% at 90 deg, and from +11.4% to +6.9% and from +12.3% to +9.0% at 30 deg.
  - **Behrendt 2008:** on the 30 deg plates the term raises the coolant flow by 3%. That is the wrong direction for the rig's coolant-flow excess, and it is reported, not tuned.
