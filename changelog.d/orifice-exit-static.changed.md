- **A plain orifice or a Cd/effective-area connection that jets into a merge chamber blows until its supply meets the chamber's static pressure (#1087).** `OrificeElement`, `EffectiveAreaConnectionElement` and `DiameterDischargeCoefficientConnectionElement` now drive as the film, effusion and slot holes do, where they are a side jet: the to-node is a `MomentumChamberNode` with `main_inlet` declared, and the element is not that main inlet.
  - **Before.** The direction came from `Pt_from - Pt_to`, so the side jet reversed wherever `P_to < Pt_from < Pt_to`, although it discharges against the static. Across `Pt_to` the flow jumped by most of a jet inside the 3 Pa blend.
  - **Now.** The direction comes from `Pt_from - P_to`. A forward flow discharges against the chamber's static. A reversed flow draws from that static, with no velocity of approach, and discharges against `Pt_from`. The flow passes zero once, continuously, and the Jacobian stays analytic through the switch. The second-law control volume, `dP_tap` and the compressible-model diagnostics read the same pair of pressures. A reversed jet's `dP_tap` was the chamber static minus the supply node's own static; it is now minus its `Pt`, for the film, effusion and slot holes too.
  - **Unchanged.** An orifice in line with a pipe, an orifice that is the chamber's `main_inlet`, one into a `MomentumChamberNode` without `main_inlet`, a recovering in-pipe metering orifice (ISO permanent loss, #645), a pass-through bore and a closed one keep the `Pt - Pt` form exactly; their residuals, Jacobians and diagnostics are bit-identical to before. Every element into a plenum or a pressure boundary is unchanged. `ImpingementPlateElement` needs no change: its node is a crossflow station, a `PlenumNode` at the channel static, so its jets already run from the supply `Pt` to that static (Florschuetz CR-3630 Eq. 4.1).
