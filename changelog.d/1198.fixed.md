- A mixer whose flowing streams all carry the same composition (one stream, or streams of one gas) returns exactly 0 for the flow partials `dY_mix/dm` (and, where the streams also share Tt or Pt, `dTt_mix/dm` and `dPt_mix/dm`) of `mixer_from_streams_and_jacobians`. They came out at about 1e-16 relative from rounding in `m q / m` and were relayed into every downstream Jacobian row as spurious entries. Mixed states and residuals are unchanged, and the other partials move by round-off only (#1198).
