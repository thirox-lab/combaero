- **Pressure-dependent WSGG gas emissivity, opt-in (#1071).** `GasRadiation(..., emissivity_model="bordbar2021")` takes eps_g from the Bordbar et al. (2021) weighted-sum-of-gray-gases model (Int. J. Heat Mass Transfer 173, 121207, CC BY 4.0) in place of Lefebvre & Ballal Eq. 8.11, on ThermalWall faces, effusion plates and liners, and in the GUI's radiation editor. Eq. 8.11 stays the default; radiation off and the default path are bit-identical.
  - **The model:** non-luminous H2O-CO2, fitted to HITEMP2010 line-by-line data, 300-3000 K, x_H2O/x_CO2 0.1-4, ten pressure sets 0.1-80 atm, read from the composition's H2O and CO2. eps is interpolated linearly in ln P between the sets, each at the actual pressure path; T, the mole ratio and eps are clamped to the fitted range.
  - **New kernels:** `cb.gas_emissivity_bordbar2021` (mole fractions) and `cb.gas_emissivity_bordbar2021_mixture` (mass fractions), with analytic partials in T, P, composition and path length, which the wall Jacobian uses; `cb.wsgg_bordbar2021_gray_gases` returns one set's gray gases for an RTE solve.
  - **Fidelity:** the paper's own slab results (Figs. 3-5, 1, 30 and 0.5 atm, 3609 points) are reproduced to max 1.25 %, mean 0.28 % (`validation/radiation/bordbar2021_slab.py`).
  - **Behrendt 2008 effusion rig (accuracy, cross-source):** new rows beside the Eq. 8.11 ones. eps_g 0.132 / 0.198 at 5 / 10 bar against 0.137 / 0.262; flat-plate eta error per vertex +8.1 % against +7.8 %, and the pressure trend closes less (0.61x against 0.89x measured); figures with the hole Cd's inclination default (#1088).
