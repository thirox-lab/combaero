- **Equilibrium combustion converges for H2 and CO fuel with a hydrocarbon (#1288).**
  - **The problem:** below about 500 K, H2 or CO fuel carrying a hydrocarbon often reported `converged=False`, and the equilibrium combustor's Jacobian raised.
    - On main, 513 of 1000 drawn H2 states with a trace of methane (0.1-6.6 % air, 150-465 K) did not converge.
    - So did 235 of 3000 drawn H2/CO states with methane to heptane. An independent, wider draw found 607 of 3000.
    - All of them converge now. `reforming_equilibrium` gains the same: 786 of 1000 and 473 of 3000 such states failed on main.
  - **Cause:** the element-potential start found the equilibrium, but its state is written back to the Newton polish as reaction extents. A species that is no reaction's key then comes out as its amount plus the extents' changes, and far below those changes it cancels.
    - CO2 at 2e-30 beside methane at 3e-5 (H2 fuel, 300 K) came back as -1e-21.
    - Steam or H2 at 1e-22 to 1e-63 beside CO came back negative likewise.
    - Which states failed depended on that rounding, so neighbouring states flipped.
  - **Now:** the joint solve takes a component basis where its scarcest non-key is more abundant than the current basis'. The most abundant independent species are the components (Smith and Missen), and every scarcer species keys its own formation from them.
  - **Jacobians:** where the old basis converged in this regime, its state carried that rounding.
    - dT/dX was 13 % off a central difference for propane in CO fuel. In a CO/pentane state it had the wrong sign (+161 against -58).
    - It now matches central differences to 2e-3 or better.
  - **No-oxygen mixtures:** some hydrocarbon traces in N2 with H2 were reported converged by main but left unreacted. They are now equilibrated, which moves T by up to 0.021 K. In a 10,000-state random draw, 15 combustion and 23 reforming results of this kind are corrected, and none gets worse.
  - **What moves elsewhere:** independent draws of 14,000 states each found no state that loses convergence or has its Jacobian newly refused.
    - Over eleven fuels and blends (150-2000 K, 1e3-1e7 Pa) and fuel-only and trace-air mixes, states move by at most 2e-12 K.
    - States of the fixed families that converged before move by at most 1.2e-11 K.
