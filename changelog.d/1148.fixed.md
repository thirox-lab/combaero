- **A solver evaluates the model a solve uses, built or solved (#1148).** `NetworkSolver.solve()` resolves the network's topology, so each component reads its neighbours: a merge chamber its inflow elements, an orifice its pipe, a crossflow segment its stations. A solver that was built but not solved, then asked for its starting point and residuals, did not resolve it. It evaluated a different model. On a `MomentumChamberNode` with a `main_inlet` and a side stream, the main face lost the side jets' momentum from the residual, and the main stream's relayed T0 and composition from the Jacobian. At the same unknowns the residual differed from the solved solver's by up to 2.8e3 (incompressible). Building the starting point now resolves the topology, and `FlowNetwork.resolve_all_topology()` runs to its fixed point. Before, a node inheriting from an element whose own geometry is inherited took it only on a later call: a GUI junction between two defaulted channels, or a chamber's area from a loss element's.
  - **An inferred chamber area is the inferred area on every solve.** A `MomentumChamberNode` or `CombustorNode` with no area of its own infers one from a neighbour, then derives its Dh from it. Every later resolve derived the area back from that Dh, an ulp off, so the reported area depended on how often the network had been resolved. The C3X burner (0.1 m^2, from its loss element) reported 0.09999999999999998 on some solves and 0.1 on others, with its `Re`, `mach`, `velocity` and `a` an ulp apart in the same way. It is now 0.1 on every solve. A chamber whose Dh an element derived from an area (an ejector's outlet chamber, a multi-port chamber's port) keeps that area the same way.
  - **Unchanged:** a `solve()` without a starting point. Its root and post-solve residual and Jacobian are bit-identical on the test suite's solves and on 640 stress draws over all 16 families, apart from run-to-run timing noise. Its reported results are bit-identical except for the inferred-area quantities above. The cooling validation runners print byte-identical output.
  - **What moves:**
    - A starting point taken from the solver before `solve()` (a user `x0` built that way) is now the resolved model's guess.
    - In the Python runner API, a `NetworkRunner` from `NetworkResult.swap_boundary()` fills the unknowns its solved state lacks from that guess: in its `solve()`, and in sweep rows that start without a previous solution. Sweep rows that start from the previous row's solution do not use it.
    - A solver that was never solved now gives the solved model's residual and Jacobian.
    - A geometry a solve refuses (an in-pipe orifice bore larger than its pipe) raises the same `ValueError` one call earlier, when the starting point is built.
  - **Related (#1142):** most of the "residual depends on solver state" difference was this, and on #1142's network solved and fresh residuals now agree to round-off. Finite-difference audits on fresh solvers used to check the unresolved model. Against the resolved one, Jacobian errors that had been hidden show up: 3.8e-3 on a reversed crossflow supply chain into an impingement array, and 2.7e-5 on a reversed chain carrying burnt gas (#1146). Neither is fixed here.
