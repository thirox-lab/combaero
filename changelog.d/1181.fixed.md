- **A film's undiluted gas takes its node's `Tt_target` heat (#1181).** When an `EnergyBoundary` with `Tt_target` sits on a film's discharge node, its solved heat `{id}.Q` now enters the undiluted gas temperature `T_inf` that the film's effectiveness is defined against. That heat is treated like Q-mode, fraction-mode and wall heat.
  - **Before:** the mix took the boundary's `Q`, which is 0 in target mode, so `T_inf` left out the solved heat. On the issue's network `T_inf` was 1494.1 K with the node held at 1350 K, against 1407.0 K for a fixed 50 kW loss.
  - **Now:** `T_inf` is 1357.8 K, bit-identical to the same network with a fixed `Q` equal to the solved heat. The Jacobian carries d(`T_inf`)/dQ (scaled FD error 2.5e-7). Networks without a target boundary on a film discharge node are unchanged.
