That held for 201 of 375 methane states between 300 and 900 K, and for 40 % of a random rich draw over six fuels. The solve now runs in a basis in which the hydrocarbon interconversions carry no oxygen species, and the scarce oxygen carriers are keys.
  - **What converges:** every methane state measured, with oxygen from 1e-13 to 1e-2 and in air up to phi 1e4, from 300 to 900 K and from 1 to 17 bar. In the random draw, 1.1 % (heptane and isobutane above phi 750) still do not converge.
  - **Agreement with Cantera:** with the same species and combaero's NASA-9 data, within 1e-7 relative in composition and 4e-5 K in temperature. Before, main was off by up to 168 K where it did not converge.
  - **States that move:** states that did converge before change by at most 1e-11 K. States that did not converge move by a median of 35 K, up to 365 K (rich burns at phi 3 to 30 move most).
  - **No Jacobian at a non-root:** `combustion_equilibrium_and_jacobian`, and through it the equilibrium combustor, now raises where the equilibrium does not converge. The network treats that as an unphysical probe.
