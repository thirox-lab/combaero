- **The incompressible reversed exit of a crossflow chain takes the duct's static density (#1136).** Reversed out of an exit node at rest (a plenum, a combustor, a boundary), the gas accelerates out of it, one dynamic head. The incompressible head took its density at the node's own P, its stagnation pressure, against its stated intent; the forward entry takes it at the duct's station static P.
  - **Before:** the reversed regime gap on a one-station bleed chain was +0.96 % at 2 kPa and +3.76 % at 8 kPa (exit Mach 0.15 / 0.31) into a plenum, +1.04 / +4.08 % into a boundary, against +0.20 / +0.73 % into a moving chamber; a K = 0 segment's two directions differed by 0.49 %.
  - **Now:** the head is taken at the static P of the duct's far end. Gap +0.17 / +0.62 % (plenum), +0.15 / +0.54 % (boundary); the K = 0 directions are mirror images to 1e-16. Only incompressible reversed exits at rest move; their flow falls by up to 3 %.
