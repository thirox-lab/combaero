- **`HoleCd`: a hole element's discharge-coefficient choices as one spec.** `combaero.network.HoleCd(correlation, Cd, inclination, inlet_crossflow, exit_crossflow)` is a frozen dataclass, passed as `hole_cd=` to `OrificeElement`, `EffusionPlateElement`, `FilmCoolingElement`, `SlotFilmElement` and `ImpingementPlateElement`, and to the `EffusionLiner` and `ImpingementArray` builders, which hand it to every panel or row (as `radiation` is handed on). The geometry stays on the element, including the exit term's `exit_crossflow_region` and `exit_crossflow_orientation_deg`.
  - **A field left at None or `"auto"` is that element's own default**, which is what it did before: correlation `"IdelchikThick"` on the effusion, film and slot elements, `"McGreehanSchotsch"` on an `EffusionLiner`'s panels, `"fixed"` (0.79) on an impingement row, `"ReaderHarrisGallagher"` (or its own `correlation` keyword) on `OrificeElement`; the inclination term on an inclined cylindrical IdelchikThick film or effusion hole; McGreehan-Schotsch's supply crossflow on a duct-fed panel; Rogers & Hersh's jet crossflow law on an impingement row and no exit term elsewhere. Every network is bit-identical (F, J and diagnostics over every stress family and the GUI examples).
  - **New choices.** `inlet_crossflow="none"` takes a duct-fed panel as plenum-fed (U1/Vi held at 0), and with it the panel is no longer refused for a correlation without the crossflow term; `"mcgreehan_schotsch"` requires a duct-fed panel and that correlation.
  - **Refusals.** The spec refuses unknown names, a non-finite `Cd` and a `Cd` with a correlation other than `"fixed"`. An element refuses a term it does not have, naming itself: the inclination and Rowbury terms on a plain orifice, a slot or an impingement row; the jet laws on a film or effusion hole; McGreehan-Schotsch's supply crossflow on a plenum-fed hole.
  - **`element.hole_cd`** is the resolved spec, and `FlowNetwork.to_dict` writes it. The refusal texts name the HoleCd field (`HoleCd(inclination='idelchik_3_2') ... does not compose`, `HoleCd(exit_crossflow=...)`), and the GUI's hint for the first now matches that text.
