The kernel formed each receiver's role weight as `m s - m`, a cancellation that kept only 1e-16 / (eps/m)^2 of its digits, and the mean supplied velocity is a ratio of such weights there: up to 7e-5 Pa of noise on 1.8e5 Pa rows, which a finite-difference check read as a Jacobian miss. The weights are now formed without the cancellation; F moves by the removed noise: up to 1.3e-4 Pa at the measured point, and up to 0.77 Pa next to the no-supplier pole (#1273). It now matches a 45-digit reference to 1.6e-11 Pa. J moves by its own round-off, about 1e-10 scaled at the point, because its weight partial cancelled the same way (#1250).
