- **A Jacobian coverage sweep in the test suite.** `python/tests/_jacobian_sweep.py` checks the network's analytic Jacobian against finite differences where the hand-written tests do not go.
  - **Networks:** the stress families, plus builders for the classes they never draw (a walled combustor among them). Each one is taken with its boundary gases, temperatures and combustor methods redrawn, and with side streams injected into its ports.
  - **Points:** every sign pattern of each element's legs (derived legs included), flows near zero on both sides of the mixer's no-flow band, and boundary-fed flows at exactly zero.
  - **Verdicts:** an entry that misses is a mismatch when the finite difference resolves the miss against its row's scale; one it cannot resolve fails above a limit unless the round-off itself bounds it. Mismatches open on main are listed narrowly (row, column, point kind, a cap).
  - **Tests:** a fast tier of fixed points that caught #1233 and #1235 before their fixes and of checks that spurious and equilibrium-only errors are flagged, and the slow tier with a coverage gate under `slow`. The gate fails if an exported element or node class, or the ThermalWall, is never reached, never reversed, or never sees a composition partial.
