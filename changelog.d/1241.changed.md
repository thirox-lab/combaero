- **The combustion equilibrium forms CH4 and NH3 where equilibrium does (#1241).** Methanation and NH3 synthesis now run wherever the burned state holds a reducing species (H2, CO, a hydrocarbon or NH3) and the elements they need, at any amount.
  - **Before:** reforming ran only above 1e-10 of burnt hydrocarbon and NH3 decomposition only above 1e-10 of NH3. Below the cut no CH4 or NH3 formed from CO, H2 and N2.
  - **Moves, against Cantera on the same species and NASA-9 data:**
    - H2/CO at phi 14 and 32.7 bar: 903.4 K before, now 1223.855 K (5.1 % CH4), Cantera 1223.855 K.
    - NH3 with CO at phi 8 and 16: 367 K and 497 K hotter than before, now within 1e-3 K of Cantera.
    - H2 at phi 4 and 100 bar: +1.65 K. CH4 at phi 2 and 100 bar: +0.44 K.
  - **Accuracy:** rich states without O2 dissociation match Cantera to 1e-3 K (155 random draws: worst 9.7e-4 K, before 5.4 K).
  - **Unchanged:** complete lean burns and fuel-free mixes, which hold no reducing species, give the same value and Jacobian as before, bit for bit.
  - **The step at the cut is gone:** a fuel trace no longer stops reforming at 1e-10, and NH3 synthesis no longer switches on in a step (up to 1.64 K at 100 bar).
