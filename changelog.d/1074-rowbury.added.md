- **`EffusionPlateElement` and `FilmCoolingElement` take `cd_exit_crossflow="rowbury_2001"`, an opt-in exit (external) crossflow term in the discharge coefficient (#1074).** It applies Rowbury, Oldfield & Lock (2001) Eqs. 7-8 on top of the no-crossflow Cd: `1/Cd^2 = 1/Cd0^2 + A k^-B - C`.
  - **The momentum flux ratio `k`.** It is built from the hole's actual flow, the surface-normal share of the jet velocity (the paper's Fig. 3) and the coolant's isentropic exit-plane density. The mainstream is the approaching one: the main inlet of the discharge `MomentumChamberNode`, at the main stream's density at the chamber's main face.
  - **Coefficients and options.** `A`, `B` and `C` come from the paper's Table 2. `exit_crossflow_region` picks the set: `"suction_surface"` (the flat-wall default), `"pressure_surface"` or `"leading_edge"`. Shaped film holes take the fan-shaped sets. `exit_crossflow_orientation_deg` is the radial orientation alpha, measured from the surface normal. The term works with any correlation, including `"fixed"`, and with or without `cd_inclination`.
  - **Crossover.** It is built in inside the fitted range: a strongly blowing hole's Cd rises.
  - **Beyond the fitted range.** The threshold for each set is the larger of two values (`rowbury_2001_k_fit_max`). The paper does not print the true fitted range, so the threshold is combaero's choice. Beyond it the `-C` offset fades, which is our extrapolation. It makes the Cd return to the no-crossflow value continuously as the mainstream vanishes.
    - **The data the paper fitted.** The largest `k` that the set's rows in the paper's Table 1 provably reach (`rowbury_2001_k_data_max`). This value sets the threshold for the pressure set (4.7), the leading edge (4.3) and both fan-shaped sets (0.13 and 0.054).
    - **The authors' own use.** The largest `k` at which the authors applied the set themselves. This value sets the threshold for the suction set: 3.8, from their Fig. 9. The suction set's data alone reach only 0.58.
    - **Diagnostic.** `exit_crossflow_faded` reports which range a hole is in: 0 inside the data, 0.5 inside only the authors' use, and 1 beyond both, where the offset fades. With the leading-edge set at a no-crossflow Cd near 1, the Cd can exceed 1.
  - **Where it does not apply.** Without a chamber with an approaching mainstream, the opt-in is refused. Below a `k` floor the term is held. The defaults are unchanged bit for bit.
  - **Diagnostics, reported only when the term is on.** The actual and the evaluated `k`, the floor, the loss, and hold, fade and range flags.
  - **GUI.** The GUI backend reads the three fields from network JSON. There is no Inspector control yet.
- **New C++ `rowbury_2001_exit_crossflow_cd`, `rowbury_2001_coefficients`, `rowbury_2001_k_perp`, `rowbury_2001_exit_density`, `rowbury_2001_k_floor` and `rowbury_2001_in_range` (`crossflow_cd.h`).** All derivatives are analytic.
- **`validation/cooling/exit_crossflow_cd_runner.py` reports fidelity and cross-source accuracy for the term.**
  - **Fidelity.** Against the authors' own Fig. 9 predictions, read by eye, the term is within 0.003 at Ma_m 0.6 and 1.2. At Ma_m 0.3 it is within 0.008 above PR 1.05.
  - **Accuracy, cross-source, with the inclination term on.** These figures come from points solved directly. A point between the mainstream static and total pressure is not reachable through the element in a network (#1087): Gritsch below PR 1.064 at Ma_m 0.3 and below PR 1.276 at Ma_m 0.6, and all of Ma_m 1.2.
    - **Gritsch 1998:** the mean error moves from +0.8% to -2.8% at Ma_m 0.3, from +15.9% to +3.2% at Ma_m 0.6, and from +46.8% to +8.6% at Ma_m 1.2.
    - **Hay 1983 at Minf 0.31, 30 deg holes:** from +11.4% to +6.8% and from +12.3% to +8.8%.
    - **Hay 1983, 90 deg holes:** from +11.2% to -4.6%. They lie outside the suction-surface set's fitted 30-78 deg, and the result depends on the floor's margin, which is combaero's own choice: between -0.2% and -9.3%.
  - **Behrendt 2008.** On the 30 deg plates (k 7.8-11.6, beyond the suction set's 3.8, so faded) the term raises the coolant flow by 1.1-1.6%. That is the wrong direction for the rig's coolant-flow excess, and it is reported, not tuned.
