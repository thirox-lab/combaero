- **A resolved or solved network can be deep-copied (#1164).** `copy.deepcopy(net)` raised `TypeError: cannot pickle ...` after a solve on every network with a thermal wall, a combustor or an effusion/film/slot/impingement hole. It also raised before a solve on any impingement network or ribbed channel.
  - **Removed write-only stores:** a node's `_wall_couplings` and a combustor's `_last_mix_res` held C++ results and were read by nothing. They are no longer written.
  - **Picklable input structs:** `OrificeGeometry`, `JetRowGeometry`, `RibGeometry`, `PinFinGeometry`, the rib, rib-ratio, jet-array, single-jet and pin-fin correlation sets, and the structs nested in them now pickle from their read-write fields. That gives them `copy.deepcopy` too.
  - **No RecursionError on copy:** the heat-transfer result wrappers' attribute forwarding recursed without end on the blank instance that copy builds. It now raises `AttributeError`.
  - **The copy is the same model:** its F and J at the solution are bit-identical to the original's.
