- **`HoleCd(supply_drive=...)`: the pressure a duct-fed hole drives from (#1087, supply side).** A duct-fed `EffusionPlateElement` (each `EffusionLiner` panel) has a duct station as its supply node, and the station holds the duct's static pressure.
  - **The choices.** `"static"` (`"auto"`, unchanged) drives from that station static, McGreehan & Schotsch's own convention. `"total"` drives from the duct's stagnation pressure at the station: the static plus the dynamic head of the mean segment flow, from C++ `hole_supply_drive` (the momentum-chamber closure, analytic partials). That is the reference of the plenum-fed Cd data. `"static"` on a hole that is not duct-fed is refused.
  - **Diagnostics.** The `Cd_*` group gains `Cd_supply_drive_model` (text), `Cd_supply_drive_applied` and `Cd_supply_drive_head_fraction`. The last is information: the duct's dynamic head over the full drive, the share the choice decides.
  - **The default is open.** Scored cross-source with McGreehanSchotsch + Eq. 17, the total drive helps co-aligned 30 deg holes (Gritsch 1998, one lab, 64 points: bias -33 % -> -8 %, RMS 43.5 % -> 43.6 %, 2 unreachable points reachable). It worsens normal holes (100 points, RMS 17.5 % -> 29.6 %), including McGreehan & Schotsch's own Fig. 6 (FIDELITY, 14.5 % -> 48.0 %). The route to a decided default is to acquire Gritsch et al. 2001 (inclination x orientation x supply crossflow) and to check whether the published inlet-crossflow correlations convert to one common drive convention.
  - **Known limitation with `"total"`.** A duct-fed panel's jet momentum into a merge chamber still takes the station's own pressure.
