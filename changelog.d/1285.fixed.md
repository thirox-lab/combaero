- **Equilibrium combustion converges for very lean, cold burns (#1285).** Below about 245 K, very lean hydrocarbon burns (phi up to about 3e-3) were refused: 29 of a 30,000-state draw from 200 to 2500 K, and 2958 of a 20,000-state draw from 150 to 260 K. Now 0 of both.
  - **How:** a trace species that is the key of no reaction, here H2 at about 1e-11 beside its fuel, is changed by the sum of the reactions' extents. A log-stepping key's extent was taken as the difference of its old and new amounts, rounded to that key's last digit; it is now computed directly. The element-potential start balanced the elements only to its corrections' 1e-10, and rebuilding the trace from the extents could leave it negative; its amounts are now balanced to rounding first. That start also hands over to Newton by NASA RP-1311's amount-weighted correction, so a trace's noise no longer refuses it.
  - **Element check:** each element is now held to 1e-12 of its own total, not of the largest one. For hydrogen in a burn at phi 1e-7 the old bound allowed about 1e-5 of its total.
  - **States that move:** states converged before move by at most 1e-11 K. Of the newly converged ones, three quarters move by less than 1e-3 K. Near-pure ethane, propane and isobutane below about 210 K (phi 2e4 to 1e6) pyrolyse, by up to 197 K (pyrolysis always, #1262).
  - **Heptane alone at zero air** now has its one-sided Jacobian; it was refused.
  - **Still not converging:** heptane with a trace of air below about 1e-20 (#1290), and H2 fuel with a trace hydrocarbon, very rich, below about 465 K (#1288).
