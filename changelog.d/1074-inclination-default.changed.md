- **The hole-angle Cd term is now on by default for cylindrical inclined holes.** `EffusionPlateElement` and `FilmCoolingElement` default to `cd_inclination="auto"`. With `correlation="IdelchikThick"` on a cylindrical hole this applies Idelchik diagram 3-2's inclined-inlet loss (#1074); shaped holes and every other correlation stay without it. `"none"` switches the term off and `"idelchik_3_2"` forces it. The element attribute holds the model that was resolved ("none" for a 90 deg hole, where the term is zero). A normal (90 deg) hole is unchanged bit for bit, and networks without inclined cylindrical IdelchikThick holes are bit-identical. On 30 deg cylindrical holes the coolant flow falls by about 10%, and the 20/30 deg effusion plates of Behrendt 2008 lose about 9%. Cross-source accuracy on Gritsch 1998 and Hay 1983, through the element: the no-crossflow bias goes from +6.4% to -4.3% (Gritsch) and from +10%/+14% to -1%/+2% (Hay carpets), and the subsonic pooled bias/RMS from +15%/29% to +8%/25% (290 clean points; the pool includes coolant- and both-crossflow cells the element has no term for, where errors partly offset). Shaped holes stay off because the term alone moves Gritsch's fan and laidback holes from -2%/0% to -11%/-10% (cross-source accuracy). With the term on, more inclined cylindrical IdelchikThick holes read `Cd_in_range = 0`: diagram 3-2's own range is Re > 1e4 and l/d >= 2, so low-Re effusion plates (Behrendt 2008's, for one) are flagged. GUI nodes default to "auto" too. A saved file that holds an explicit `"none"` keeps it.
- **Behrendt 2008 (`validation/cooling/effusion_network_runner.py`) has been re-scored with the new default.** Every plateau eta falls by 0.4-0.9 points. The mean over-prediction goes from +21.6% to +21.1% on the chamber baseline and from +12.5% to +11.5% on the flat-plate baseline; with radiation, per vertex, it is +16.8% and +7.8%.
