- **A crossflow segment's Jacobian carries the composition partials of the gas each term reads (#1146).** The C++ kernels behind the segment's momentum terms (`station_half_drop`, `channel_entry_drop`, `segment_area_change_drop`) and a joining jet's axial momentum (`jet_impulse`) had no derivative in the composition. A gas whose composition moves with the flows therefore lacked d/dY in every crossflow row that read it. Such a gas comes from a burning `CombustorNode`, from a plenum mixing two streams, or from a station where a jet of another gas joins. The residuals are unchanged, bit for bit; only the Jacobian moves.
  - **New fields:** `StationHalfDrop.d_dY` and `JetImpulse.dJ_dY`, the derivatives in the mass fractions at fixed (P, T) and at fixed (Pt, Tt, P), one entry per species. The jet's are analytic on both the unchoked and the choked branch.
  - **Each partial is keyed to the node that supplies the gas.** That node is the reservoir for the entry term (#1137) and the lossless entry from a node at rest (#1150). It is the term's own node for the station halves and the reversed exit (`exit_in`), the upstream end for the taper's Bernoulli term, the carried gas's node for the exit head into a `MomentumChamberNode`, and the jet's supply for its momentum. The same `jet_impulse` partial reaches a merge chamber's side jets.
  - **A reversed angled jet into a crossflow station is keyed to its real supply.** An `OrificeElement` declared station -> combustor whose flow reverses jets the combustor's gas into the station. Its momentum partials were keyed to the declared ends: the flow with the wrong sign, the supply's Pt and T0 (and now Y) on the station, and the discharge P on the combustor. They now follow the stream the solver relays: its supply node, its flow columns, and the node it discharges into. The worst row of such a chain falls from 5.3e-2 incompressible and 6.9e-2 compressible to <= 1.7e-8. A forward jet's partials are unchanged, bit for bit.
  - **FD error before -> after** (worst row, scaled J_ij |x_j|, fresh solver, at the root, perturbed and at m = +-1e-7):
    - a burning combustor feeding an entry segment, a merge chamber or a stationed chain: up to 1.0e-3 incompressible and 3.1e-4 compressible -> <= 2.0e-8;
    - a plenum mixing air with a CH4-bearing stream in the same place: up to 3.6e-4 -> <= 1.6e-8; with a 30 deg jet whose own supply plenum mixes two gases, up to 9.0e-4 incompressible and 1.3e-3 compressible -> <= 1.4e-8; the same jet choked at the root, 6.9e-4 / 7.7e-4 -> <= 1.9e-8;
    - the same networks reversed: up to 6.8e-5 -> <= 8.0e-8;
    - reversed flow out of a burning combustor through an entry segment (#1137's networks): 5.9e-4 to 7.2e-4 incompressible, through the reversed exit term `exit_in` -> <= 1.1e-8; the reversed compressible chain u0 -> station -> u1 from that combustor: 2.7e-5 -> 1.2e-8 on every row.
  - **Unchanged:** every residual (3036 evaluations over the 16 stress families, seeds 0:40, the GUI examples and the #1137/#1146 test networks). Where the composition is uniform the Jacobian moves by round-off only (<= 2.2e-16 of a row's largest entry), through the composition relay's (Y_i - Y_mix) terms.
