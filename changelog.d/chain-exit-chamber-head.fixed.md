- An incompressible `CrossflowSegmentElement` / `ImpingementCrossflowElement` chain whose exit segment (no to-station) discharges into a `MomentumChamberNode` now carries the duct's exit head into the chamber: the duct's exit total pressure meets the chamber's `Pt`, as the compressible segment already did. Before, the exit static met the chamber's `Pt`, so the head was lost and the regimes differed by 4-5 % in flow on an impingement array whatever the exit Mach (far more on a reversed exit); they now differ as on a plenum exit, O(Ma^2). Exits into a plenum or boundary, which lose the head by convention, and the compressible residuals are unchanged.
