- **A solver evaluates the model a solve uses, built or solved (#1148).** `NetworkSolver.solve()` resolves the network's topology, so each component reads its neighbours: a merge chamber its inflow elements, an orifice its pipe, a crossflow segment its stations. A solver that was only built, then had its starting point taken (`_build_x0()`) and its residuals evaluated, did not resolve it. It evaluated a different model. On a `MomentumChamberNode` with a `main_inlet` and a side stream, the main face lost the side jets' momentum from the residual and the main stream's relayed T0 and composition from the Jacobian. At the same unknowns, the residual differed from the solved solver's by up to 2.8e3 (incompressible). `_build_x0()` now resolves the topology, which every residual and Jacobian evaluation goes through.
  - **Unchanged:** a `solve()` without a starting point. Its root, results and post-solve residual and Jacobian are bit-identical on every test network and stress family; the cooling validation runners are byte-identical.
  - **What moves:** a starting point taken from `_build_x0()` before `solve()`, which is now the resolved model's guess. In the GUI this is a sweep's warm start, for unknowns the warm state does not hold. Any residual or Jacobian evaluated on a solver that was never solved also moves: such evaluations now give the solved model's numbers, and a network a solve would refuse (an orifice bore larger than its pipe) is refused there too.
  - **Related (#1142):** most of the "residual depends on solver state" difference was this, and on #1142's network solved and fresh F now agree to round-off. FD audits on fresh solvers used to check the unresolved model. Against the resolved one they now show Jacobian errors that had been hidden there: 3.8e-3 on a reversed crossflow supply chain into an impingement array, and 2.7e-5 on a reversed chain fed with burnt gas (#1146). Neither is fixed here.
