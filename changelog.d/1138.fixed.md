- **A bare `CrossflowSegmentElement` now loses its exit head the way a `ChannelElement` does (#1138).** A bare segment has no station at either end, counting a station that a neighbouring segment registers there, and no `entry_K`.
  - **Before:** such a segment never paid its exit dynamic head into a `PressureBoundary`, in either flow direction or regime. The opt-out of #588 covered every segment, although its reason (station nodes discharge at static continuity) only holds for a segment with a station or an entry.
    - In a two-element network (the bare segment and a `ChannelElement` in series between two boundaries 2 kPa apart, roughness 0), the segment discharging into a boundary passed 3.88e-2 kg/s incompressible and 3.66e-2 compressible. With a channel in its place the network passed 1.2847e-2: 3.0x, and a regime gap of +6.0 %.
    - Alone between the two boundaries it passed 4.83e-2 against 1.32e-2 (3.7x, +8.0 %).
  - **Now:** its exit follows the `ChannelElement`'s. The head is lost into a `PressureBoundary` whether the segment discharges forward into its to-node or reversed into its from-node. Its flow is the channel's to 1e-6, and the regime gap is +0.18 % (+0.16 % alone). Its report (`exit_head_recovered`, the end states) is also the channel's.
  - **Unchanged:** into a `PlenumNode` or a `MomentumChamberNode`, where a channel recovers the head, the bare segment is unchanged. So are segments with a station or an `entry_K`, and a segment leaving a station that its neighbour registers, whose residuals and Jacobians this change leaves bit-identical (#1150 then adds the head on such a segment's boundary-fed leg). The last case is fed at the station's static pressure with its gas already moving, so charging the head there would count it twice.
  - **Who is affected:** no shipped builder (impingement gap, `EffusionLiner`) creates a bare segment, so only hand-built ones move.
