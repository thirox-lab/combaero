- **The ejector's Jacobian follows its secondary gas (#1246).** gamma and R in `EjectorElement` are the secondary port gas's, and that gas moves with the flows: a reversed leg delivers the ejector's own mix to the port chamber, a side stream changes it, and its T0 moves the choke-plane gamma even for one gas.
  - **Before:** both were held frozen in the Jacobian, so its rows missed their finite difference with a leg reversed: 0.9 % on the flow row (ejector:2/v1/s0), up to 1.1e-2 scaled across the ejector sweep, single gas included.
  - **Now:** the C++ kernel returns each row's partials in gamma and R (`d_dgamma`, `d_dr_gas` on `EjectorElementResidualJacobian`, through the nozzle-exit Mach's implicit derivative too), and the element chains them onto the secondary node's T0 and composition.
- **The ejector's C-D nozzle near zero drive.** Its subsonic exit Mach, M^2 = 2/(g-1) (r^(-(g-1)/g) - 1), cancelled as r = P_py/Pt -> 1, a relative error of about eps/(1 - r) in the nozzle flow and its P_py slope (1.5e-7 at 1 - r = 1e-9, 1e-4 at 1e-12). It is now evaluated with expm1/log1p, at rounding throughout. The element's flow rows move by at most 1.9e-12 of their scale; every other residual and closure value is unchanged, bit for bit.
